- **anodyne**
    
    *noun/adjective*
    
    noun: something that soothes or relieves pain
    
    **The medicine had an anodyne effect.
    → It relieved pain.**
    
    🧠 anodyne = i no dying = no pain = relieves a pain
    
    adjective: inoffensive
    
    🧠 anodyne = when you’re on anesthesia, you give offensive comments (due to drugs), but without it (i no dying), you give inoffensive comments to nurse
    
    **The politician gave an anodyne speech that avoided controversial topics.
    → A bland, inoffensive speech.**
    
    **His comments were deliberately anodyne.
    → His comments were safe and unlikely to upset anyone.**
