# Part 45

- **abject**
    
    *adjective*
    
    Meaning 1: extremely bad, miserable, or hopeless ⇒ dejected
    
    Meaning 2: completely lacking dignity or pride
    
    syn: **wretched; deplorable; miserable; pitiful; pitiable**
    
    **abject conditions**
    
    **abject poverty**
    
    🧠 abject = an object on the ground
    
    - no dignity
    - no pride
    - complete misery
    
    abject = reduced to the lowest condition.
    
- **amicable**
    
    *adjective*
    
    friendly and cooperative; showing goodwill, especially in settling disputes.
    
    syn: **congenial; convivial; cordial**
    
    🧠 amicable = amiable
    
- **aver**
    
    *verb*
    
    to state, declare or assert with confidence
    
    syn: **attest; avow**
    
    ant: **abjure**
    
- **avow**
    
    *verb*
    
    syn: **attest; aver; affirm**
    
    ant: **disavow**
    
    | **Feature** | **Avow** | **Disavow** |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Core Action** | To claim or admit | To reject or deny |
    | **Main Goal** | To take ownership | To break ties |
    | **Common Synonyms** | Affirm, confess, assert | Disclaim, repudiate, reject |
- **affirm**
    
    *verb*
    
    to state clearly and publicly that something is true, or to give formal support to an idea, rule, or decision
    
    🧠 affirm = a firm confirmation
