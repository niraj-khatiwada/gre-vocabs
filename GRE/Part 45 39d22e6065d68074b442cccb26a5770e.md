- **abject**
    
    *adjective*
    
    Meaning 1: extremely bad, miserable, or hopeless
    
    Meaning 2: completely lacking dignity or pride
    
    syn: **wretched; deplorable; miserable; pitiful; pitiable**
    
    **abject conditions**
    
    **abject poverty**
    
    🧠 abject = object on the ground
    
    - no dignity
    - no pride
    - complete misery
    
    abject = reduced to the lowest condition.
    
- **amicable**
    
    *adjective*
    
    friendly and cooperative; showing goodwill, especially in settling disputes.
    
    syn: **congenial; convivial; cordial**
    
    🧠 amicable = amiable
    
- **aver**
    
    *verb*
    
    to state, declare or assert with confidence
    
    syn: **attest; avow**
    
    ant: **abjure**
    
- **avow**
    
    *verb*
    
    syn: **attest; aver**
    
    ant: **disavow**
    
- **cathartic**
    
    *adjective*
    
    providing emotional release, cleansing, or relief after expressing strong emotions ⇒ emotionally cleanse (you cry a lot  after stress and you feel good)
    
    syn: **purgative** (derived from purge); **purifying**; **exorcising** (derived from exorcism = exorcism means to evict demons from your body)
    
    syn: cathartic = cat + therapy = cleanse your emotions

- **fortitude**
