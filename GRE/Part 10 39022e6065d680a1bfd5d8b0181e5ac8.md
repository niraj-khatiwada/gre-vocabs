# Part 10

- **forsake**
    
    *verb*
    
    to abandon, completely give up; to leave someone or something forever, especially when you have a duty to stay
    
    syn: **abjure; disavow**
    
- **forbear**
    
    *verb*
    
    syn**: restrain; refrain; abstain**; to hold back
    
    🧠 forbear =  for bear 🐻 = imagine a bear is trying to attack you and you hold back
    
- **approbation**
    
    *noun*
    
    approval, appreciation or endorsement towards someone
    
    🧠 **appro**bation = **appro**val or praise
    
    **I was hoping for some kind of approbation after finishing the project, but it appears my boss doesn't care. He is always busy with other tasks.**
    
- **corroborate**
    
    *verb*
    
    confirm, support, strengthen or lend support to a claim
    
    back up a claim with proof
    
    syn: **validate**
    
    🧠 co-robot 🤖: imagine there are 2 robots. 
    
    👮 Detective: "What happened?"
    
    🤖 Robot 1: "The suspect left at 8 PM."
    
    🤖 Robot 2: "Yes, exactly."
    
    Robot 2 **corroborates** Robot 1's story.
    
- **substantiate**
    
    *verb*
    
    verify something by providing evidence
    
    syn: **corroborate**
    
- **disparate**
    
    *adjective*
    
    different; separate; dissimilar; contrasting
    
    syn: **heterogeneous**
    
    🧠 disparate = dis + separate = this is separate
    
- **emulate**
    
    *verb*
    
    act of imitating or copying something
    
    syn: **imitate; mirror; mimic**
    
    🧠 emulate = emulation → simulation (imagine iPhone simulator)→ clone
    
- **enervate**
    
    *verb*
    
    drain someone's energy or strength.
    
    syn: **weaken; exhaust**
    
    🧠 enervate =  E = Energy → NERVE → No energy
    
    🧠 enervated = sounds like energized but it’s opposite
    
- **ephemeral**
    
    *adjective*
    
    extremely short-lasting
    
    syn: **transitory; fleeting**
    
    🧠 ephemeral = E-FEw-Moments” = a few moments
    
- **fervid**
    
    *adjective*
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH furtive
    
    Intensely emotional, passionate, or enthusiastic.
    
    Showing strong feeling, often in speech, belief, or expression.
    
    strong, heated emotion
    
    🧠 fervid **≈ fire + vivid emotion**
    
    🧠 fervid = derived from word fervor = intense passion or enthusiasm
    
- **fervent**
    
    *adjective*
    
    **fervid** and **fervent** both has 2 (similar) meaning:
    
    Meaning 1: deeply passionate and enthusiastic about something
    
    Meaning 2: intense emotion or warmth; showing intense emotion usually in speech
    
    syn: **fervid**
    
- **ardent**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **fervid; fervent**
    
    🧠 ardent = derived from ardor
    
- **zealous**
    
    *adjective*
    
    someone who is deeply passionate, enthusiastic, and devoted to a cause, belief, or activity
    
    syn: **fervid, ardent**
    
    🧠 **zeal**ous = derived from zeal = zeal means great enthusiasm, interest or passion towards something
    
    ---
    
    A zealot is a person who is intensely passionate about something.
    
- **garrulous**
    
    *adjective*
    
    talkative; wordy
    
    syn: **verbose, loquacious, chatty**
    
    🧠 garrulous **= garr** = “Grrr… too much talking” 😤
    
- **ascertain**
    
    *verb*
    
    find out or discover something for certain; to confirm that something is certain; make sure what is true
    
    🧠 ascertain **=** make **certain**
    
    So:
    
    - You investigate
    - You check evidence
    - You confirm truth
    
    That’s **ascertain**.
    
- **unscathed**
    
    *adjective*
    
    not injured, harmed or damaged in any way
    
    🧠 unscathed = **un-scratched** = not harmed

- **expend**
    
    *verb*
    
    to use up resource like time, money, energy, etc.
    
    🧠 expend = spend = spend resource
    
- **cherish**
    
    *verb*
    
    deeply care or admire about something; protect and appreciate something emotionally important.
    
    syn: **treasure**; **value; adore; appreciate; admire**
    
    🧠 cherish = care + rich
    
    **cherish your life**
    
- **treasure**

    noun = **accumulated wealth, such as money, jewels, or precious metals**

    🧠 treasure = we deeply care about our treasure/jwellery
