- **hysterical**
    
    *adjective*
    
    Meaning 1: hilariously funny
    
    Meaning 2: extreme emotions (like panic or weeping); exaggerated in reaction
    
    syn: **delirious; feverish**
    
    🧠 hysterical = oh my god! that was hysterical
    
- **delirious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    1. confused / mentally disturbed
        - **The patient became delirious because of a high fever.**
    2. wildly excited / overjoyed
        - **Fans were delirious with excitement after the victory.**
    
    Core idea: mind is overwhelmed and not functioning normally ⇒ extreme emotions
    
    🧠 delirious = daily+rush = you have extreme emotions due to daily rush
    
    🧠 delirious = the brain is deliring (delaying clear thinking)
