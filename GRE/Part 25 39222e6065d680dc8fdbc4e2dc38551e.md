- **postulate**
    
    *verb*
    
    assume something as true even without full proof; use the available evidence as a starting point to prove in the future
    
    Core idea: **assume first → prove later;** done by scientist and scholars
    
    syn: **hypothesize; assume; theorize**
    
    🧠 postulate = post it note 📌 
    
    You write an idea on a **Post-it note** and stick it on the wall.
    
    You haven't proved it yet.
    
    You're saying:
    
    > "Let's assume this is true and build from here."
    > 
    
    👉 That's a **postulate**.
