- **teeter**
    
    *verb*
    
    to wobble, sway, or be on the verge of falling or failing because of instability.
    
    Core idea: barely keeping balance.
    
    🧠 teeter
    
    Teeter = Totter 🤸
    
    Think of a toddler learning to walk:
    
    🚶‍♂️↔️
    
    They go:
    
    Left... Right... Left... Right...
    
    Almost falling!
    
    That wobbling is teetering.
    
    Teeter = wobble on the edge of falling.
    
    **The child teetered on the edge of the curb.
    → The child wobbled and almost fell.**
    
    **The company teeters on the brink of bankruptcy.
    → The company is close to failing.**
