- **incidental**
    
    *adjective*
    
    Meaning 1: happening by chance
    
    Meaning 2: non-essential part of something; secondary; peripheral ⇒ imagine main event is primary but incidental event (happened by chance) is secondary/peripheral
    
    syn: **auxiliary; peripheral; secondary**
    
- **ancillary**
    
    *noun/adjective*
    
    something that is secondary, supplementary, or supporting the main thing, rather than being the main thing itself.
    
    syn: **incidental; secondary; auxiliary; peripheral**
    
    🧠 ancillary = a+new+culinary = secondary dish from your main option

- **lugubrious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    extremely sad, gloomy, or mournful.
    
    🧠 lugubrious = lull(quiet)+gloomy(sad) = sad and gloomy
    
    **The funeral had a lugubrious atmosphere.
    → The atmosphere was very sad and mournful.**
    
    **He spoke in a lugubrious voice about his failures.
    → His voice sounded gloomy and sorrowful.**
    
    **The movie's lugubrious tone made it feel depressing.
    → The mood was dark and sad.**
