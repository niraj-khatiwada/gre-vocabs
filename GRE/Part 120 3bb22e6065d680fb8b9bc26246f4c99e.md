- **consilience**
    
    *noun*
    
    the linking together of principles from different disciplines especially when forming a comprehensive theory
    
    Core Idea: different paths → same conclusion.
    
    🧠 consilience = con(come together)+silence
    
    Imagine several scientists who have been working independently.
    
    They finally compare their results—and everyone goes **silent** because the evidence all points to the same answer.
    
    **CON + SILENCE → different evidence converges → CONSILIENCE**

- **consternation**
    
    *noun*
    
    a feeling of sudden anxiety, shock, confusion, or dismay caused by something unexpected or alarming.
    
    Core Idea: “Oh no! What just happened?”
    
    syn: **dismay; alarm; distress; anxiety; apprehension; agitation; bewilderment; perturbation; trepidation**
    
    🧠 consternation = concern+tarnation(tarnation means shock or confusion) = you’re concerned about what just happened
    
    **The sudden resignation of the company's director caused considerable consternation among the employees.**
    
    **The announcement was met with consternation by citizens who had expected the policy to remain unchanged.**
