- **subordinate**
    
    *noun/adjective*
    
    having a lower rank; having little importance
    
    syn: **inferior; peripheral**
    
    🧠 subordinate = sub+ordinate = lower co-ordinate in xy coordinates = lower rank = less important
    
    **In the military hierarchy, junior officers are subordinate to senior commanders.**
    
    → lower rank
    
    **The researcher considered theoretical elegance subordinate to empirical evidence.**
    
    → less important
    
    **The manager expected her subordinates to follow the new procedures without question.**
    
    → lower rank employees

- **promulgate**

    🧠 **prom**ulg**ate** = promote + circulate
    
- **ruffle**
    
    *verb*
    
    to disturb, annoy, upset, or distress someone
    
    🧠 ruffle = rough+scuffle = scuffle means a messy fight = disturb or distress
    
    **The criticism did not ruffle her confidence.
    → It did not upset her.**
    
    **The wind ruffled his hair.
    → It made his hair messy.**
    
    **The unexpected question ruffled the politician.
    → It disturbed his composure.**
