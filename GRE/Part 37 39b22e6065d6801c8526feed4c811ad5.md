- **complementary**
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH compl**i**mentary(notice ”i”: derived from compliment)
    
    *adjective*
    
    matched well; harmonious; compatible; balanced
    
    🧠 complement = something that completes
    
    🧠 remember “i” and “e” version of compl**i**mentary and compl**e**mentary
    
    complementary with e is **something that** **completes**
    
    complimentary with i is  **given free of charge, or expressing praise and admiration**
