- **vicissitude**
    
    *noun*
    
    a change or variation in circumstances, especially a difficult or unpleasant change.
    
    vicissitude = vicious shifts = 😊 success ⇒ 😞 failure ⇒ 😊 recovery = These changing situations are **vicissitudes**.
    
    **He experienced many vicissitudes during his career.
    → He went through many changes and difficulties.**
    
    **The novel explores the vicissitudes of human relationships.
    → The changing and unpredictable nature of relationships.**
    
    **She remained optimistic despite the vicissitudes of life.
    → She stayed positive despite life's ups and downs.**
    
- **expunge**
    
    *verb*
    
    to erase, remove, or completely eliminate something
    
    syn: **eradicate; purge; obliterate**
    
    🧠 expunge = erase + purge = remove completely
    
    **The court agreed to expunge his criminal record.
    → The record was officially erased.**
    
    **The editor expunged unnecessary details from the article.
    → The details were removed.**
    
    **The company tried to expunge all traces of the error.
    → It tried to remove all evidence of the mistake.**
