# Atomic Habits

- **adamant**
    
    *adjective*
    
    refusing to change one’s mind
    
    synonym: **tenacious**
    
    **“Laszlo’s wife was not as adamant as him regarding training their kids from the childhood.”**
    
- **meticulous**
    
    *adjective*
    
    showing great attention to detail; very careful and precise
    
    synonym: **fastidious, painstaking**
    
    **“Laszlo kept a meticulous file system to track their children’s history with the competitors.”**
    
- **atypical**
    
    *noun*
    
    abnormal; not typical
    
    synonym: **anomalous**
    
    **“Their childhood was atypical to say the least.”**
    
- **unbeknownst**
    
    *adverb*
    
    without the knowledge of (usually used in front of “to”)
    
    **“Unbeknownst to me, my wife surprised me while I arrived at home early.”**
    
- **bewilder**
    
    *verb*
    
    cause to confuse someone because of something complex or difficult to understand.
    
    synonym: **baffle**
    
    **“Those questions bewildered even the experts.”**
    
- **atrophy**
    
    *verb*
    
    weaken or **degenerate** (usually body parts)
    
    **“Cells that are not used for a long time will atrophy.”**
    
- **sap**
    
    *verb*
    
    deplete or weaken
    
    **“sap our time”**
    
    **“sap our stamina”**
    
    **“sap our savings”**
    
- **hone**
    
    *verb*
    
    to refine or make sharp (तिखो बनाउनु)
    
    **“hone your skills.”
    ”hone your thinking.”**
    
- **gravitate**
    
    *verb*
    
    incline towards; give one more favor over other.
    
    synonym: **incline**
    
    **“We tend to gravitate towards tasks that are easy and take less time to complete.”**
    
- **decisive**
    
    *adjective*
    
    ability to make a decision more quickly and effectively
    
    **“cast the decisive vote”**
    
    **“decisive influence”**
    
    **“decisive moments”**
    
- **concoct**
    
    *verb*
    
    invent
    
    **“I concocted a solid plan to beat my procrastination.”**
    
- **mulled**
    
    *verb*
    
    think deeply about a subject
    
    synonym: **contemplate**
    
    **“Patterson mulled over his predicament and finally came to a conclusion.“**
    
- **predicament**
    
    *noun*
    
    a difficult, unpleasant, or embarrassing situation
    
    **“I’m going through a financial predicament right now.”**
    
- **whim**
    
    *noun*
    
    a sudden desire
    
    **“He bought the book on a whim.”**
    
- **expanse**
    
    *noun*
    
    a wide scope
    
    **“We can splurge into the vast expanse of social media these days from a whim.”**
    
- **prime**
    
    *verb*
    
    make something ready for use or action.
    
    **“A good way to build a new habit is by priming the environment you are currently in.”**
    
- **amenity**
    
    *noun*
    
    things that make you comfortable or at ease
    
    **“All the amenities of home.”**
    
    **“I never knew reading books was my amenity.”**
    
- **squatter**
    
    *noun*
    
    someone who occupies land without permission (सुकुमबासि)
    
    **“A lot of people live in squatter settlements in Kathmandu.”**
    
- **makeshift**
    
    *adjective*
    
    done or made using whatever is available.
    
    **“We immediately built a makeshift house to host the refugees.”**
    
- **stagnant**
    
    *adjective*
    
    not circulating or flowing (जमेको पानि)
    
    **“stagnant water”**
    
- **pamper**
    
    *verb*
    
    treat or indulge with excessive comfort, attention or kindness.
    
    **“Rich people like being pampered.”**
    
    **“Grandparents often pamper their grandchildren.”**
    
- **frivolous**
    
    *adjective*
    
    not having any serious purpose or value
    
    **“Do not get indulge into frivolous purchases.”**
    
    **“frivolous time period.”**
    
    **“frivolous tasks.”**
    
- **timid**
    
    *adjective*
    
    lack of self-confidence, boldness or courage
    
    antonym: **audacious**
    
    **“I was too timid to ask for what I wanted.”**
    
    ---
    
    *noun*
    
    **“The adventure of rafting is not for a timid.”**
    
- **elite**
    
    *adjective*
    
    selected as best
    
    **“elite coaches”**
    
    **“elite colleges”**
    
- **rationalize**
    
    *verb*
    
    think or explain rationally; think or explain logically and with reasoning
    
    **“She couldn’t rationalize her behavior on the wedding day.”** 
    
- **derail**
    
    *verb*
    
    obstruct or hinder by diverting from its intended course or path
    
    **“The new “Snow White“ movie’s plot derails the Hollywood’s ability to make good cinemas.”**
    
- **complacency**
    
    *noun*
    
    the feeling you have when you are satisfied with yourself
    
    **“His complacency was absolutely disgusting.”**
    
    **“Notwithstanding the fact that the team lost, his complacency was completely unnecessary.”**
    
- **trumpet**
    
    *verb*
    
    proclaim widely or loudly
    
    **“The media trumpeted the death of the queen the very next day.”**
    
- **proclaim**
    
    *verb*
    
    announce publicly or officially
    
    **“They proclaimed that the wildfire will be stopped sooner than expected.”**
    
- **latch**
    
    *noun*
    
    चुकुल

    *verb*
    
    fasten with latch
    
    **“Can you latch the door quickly?”**
    
- **impermanent**
    
    *adjective*
    
    not lasting
    
    **synonym**: temporary
    
- **rattle**
    
    *verb*
    
    ढ्याङ्ग ढ्याङ्ग ढ्याङ्ग
    
    make or cause to make a rapid succession of short, sharp knocking sounds.
    
    **“The wind caused the roof to rattle.”**
    
    **“The door rattled.”**
