- **obviate**
    
    *verb*
    
    sidestep/prevent the potential problem; to remove a need
    
    > It is similar to forestall but in this case, we might sidestep the potential problem. In forestall, we preemptively prevent the problem. But, obviate can mean forestall as well.
    > 
    
    syn: **avert, circumvent, bypass; curb**
    
    🧠 ob**viate** = deviate = sidestep
    
    **The new software update obviates the need for manual data entry.**
    
    → removes the need
