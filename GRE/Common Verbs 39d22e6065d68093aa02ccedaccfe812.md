# Common Verbs

- **qualify**
    
    *verb*
    
    Meaning 1: To meet the standard
    
    Meaning 2: to lessen the severity of something; make less harsh
    
    syn: **mollify; pacify**
    
    🧠 qualify = sounds similar to mollify, pacify
    
- **entertain**
    
    *verb*
    
    Meaning 1: to provide someone with amusement or enjoyment
    
    Meaning 2: to consider the possibility of 
    
    **The manager refused to entertain the possibility of delaying the project.**
    
- **delineate**
    
    *verb*
    
    to describe, outline, or define something with precision and clarity
    
    🧠 delineate = line by line = clear and thorough outline
    
- **canvass**
    
    *verb*
    
    to solicit votes, opinions, or sales, usually by going door-to-door or surveying the public
    
- **stipulate**
    
    *verb*
    
    demand a requirement as a part of the agreement; to specify or require something as a condition or part of an agreement.
    
    ***“We’ll agree, but ONLY under these conditions.”***
    
    syn: **demand; press(for)**
    
    🧠 stipulate = agree to these terms to get stipend
    
    **The grant stipulated several conditions that the researchers had to satisfy before receiving the funds.**
    
    ---
    
    *noun*
    
    stipulation
    
- **surmise**
    
    *verb*
    
    form an opinion without absolute proof; to guess based on limited evidence
    
    syn: **presume; assume**
    
- **conjecture**
    
    *verb*
    
    without firm evidence
    
    syn: **speculate**
    
- **speculate**
    
    *verb*
    
    guess
    
- **allude**
    
    *verb*
    
    to suggest, hint at, or make an indirect reference to something without explicitly stating or directly mentioning it
    
    **The judge alluded…**

    *adjective*
    
    allusive
