- **contentious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    🧠 contentious = derived from contend = argument/dispute
    
- **polemical**
    
    *adjective*
    
    involving strong argument, controversy, or debate; giving hostile opinion
    
    syn: **argumentative, controversial, disputatious, contentious, combative, disputative**
    
    🧠 polemical = political = argument or hostile opinions
    
    🧠 polemical = polar opposite = hostile
    
    ---
    
    *noun*
    
    polemic
    
- **vitriolic**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **polemical; contentious**
    
    🧠 vitriolic = derived from vitriol = harsh opinions or comments; hostile opinions
    
    ---
    
    *noun*
    
    vitriol
    
- **vehement**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **polemical; contentious; vitriolic**
    
- **vitriol**
