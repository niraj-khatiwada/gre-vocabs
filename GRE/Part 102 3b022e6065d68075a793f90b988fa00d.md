- **moribund**
    
    *adjective*
    
    dying, close to death, or in a state of decline with little chance of recovery ⇒ almost dead
    
    🧠 moribund = mortal+bound = bound to death = more ma more
    
    **The patient was moribund by the time the doctors arrived.
    → The patient was close to death.**
    
    **The once-thriving company became moribund.
    → The company was dying economically.**
    
    **The tradition is now moribund.
    → The tradition is nearly extinct.**
    
- **cede**
    
    *verb*
    
    to give up control or possession; surrender; handover; yield
    
    🧠 cede = con**cede =** give up; surrender
    
    - **The country was forced to cede territory after the war.
    → It gave up land.**
    - **She refused to cede authority to her deputy.
    → She would not give up power.**
    - **The company agreed to cede its rights to the patent.
    → It transferred its rights.**

- **semblance**
    
    *adjective*
    
    an outward appearance, resemblance, or a small amount of something that makes it seem real.
    
    Core Idea: acting as if it is but it’s misleading
    
    🧠 semblance = same+resemblance = seems similar but is not
    
    **The two paintings bear a semblance to each other. → They resemble each other.**
    
    **His apology had a semblance of sincerity. → It looked somewhat sincere, but may not have been genuine.**
    
    **The meeting restored some semblance of order. → It brought back an appearance/degree of order.**

- **maunder**
    
    *verb*
    
    Meaning 1: (physically) wander around aimlessly
    
    Meaning 2: (speech) speak aimlessly; wander with words
    
    Core Idea: both means to wander aimlessly
    
    🧠 maunder = meander (🧠 remember meander?)
    
    🧠 maunder = me+wander = wander aimlessly
    
    **The professor maundered for an hour without making a clear point.
    → He talked aimlessly.**
    
    **The old man maundered about his childhood memories.
    → He rambled on.**
    
    **The essay maunders instead of presenting a clear argument.
    → It lacks focus.**

- **bowdlerize**
    
    *verb*
    
    to remove or edit parts of a text, speech, or work that are considered offensive, vulgar, immoral, or inappropriate.
    
    syn: **expurgate**
    
    🧠 bowdlerize = bowel(intestine)+sanitize = clean your vulgar shit
    
    **The movie was bowdlerized for television. → Offensive scenes or language were edited out.**
    
    **The publisher bowdlerized the novel before releasing it to schools. → It removed controversial material.**
    
- **expurgate**
    
    *verb*
    
    to remove parts of a text, speech, or work that are considered offensive, inappropriate, or objectionable.
    
    Core Idea: remove bad parts
    
    🧠 expurgate = extract+purge = extract bad things and purge them
    
    **The expurgated sex scenes for television.
    → Content was edited out.**
    
    **The publisher expurgated offensive language from the novel.
    → Removed inappropriate words.**
    
    **The government report was expurgated before being released.
    → Certain parts were removed.**
