# Part 38

- **impugn**
    
    *verb*
    
    try to challenge or argue or criticize
    
    syn: **dispute; contest; combat; argue**
    
    🧠 im**pugn = pugn**acious = verb form of pugnacious = attack or combat or argue or criticize
    
    **Though many initially tried to impugn Darwin's theory, in scientific circles today, the idea is taken as truth.**
    
- **inveterate**
    
    *adjective*
    
    firmly established through long practice; deeply ingrained; unlikely to change
    
    syn: **ingrained; entrenched; hardened**
    
    🧠 inveterate = veteran = established and hardened belief
    
    **inveterate gambler**
