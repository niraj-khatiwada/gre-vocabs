- **feckless**

    syn: **inept; ineffectual**
    
    🧠 feckless = effect less = ineffective
    
    **feckless leader**
    
    **Two years after graduation, Charlie still lived with his parents and had no job, becoming more feckless with each passing day.**

- **indigent**

- **destitute**
    
    *adjective*
    
    lacking; wanting (can be money or any other resources)
    
    syn: **penurious; parsimonious; tightfisted**
    
    🧠 destitute = **des**perate+pros**titute** = lacking money
    
    **After losing his job and home, he became destitute.
    → He had almost nothing.**
    
    **The charity helps destitute families.
    → Families living in extreme poverty.**
    
    **The war left thousands of people destitute.
    → People were left without basic needs.**

- **exegesis**
    
    *noun*
    
    critical and detailed explanation or analysis, especially of a text
    
    🧠 exegesis = **ex**plain+**thesis** = explain in detail
    
    **The professor wrote an exegesis of Shakespeare's play.
    → A detailed analysis and interpretation of the play.**
    
    **The scholar's exegesis of the ancient manuscript revealed new meanings.
    → A careful explanation of the manuscript.**
    
    **The priest delivered an exegesis of a biblical passage.
    → A detailed interpretation of the passage.**
    
- **defray**
    
    *verb*
    
    to pay for, cover, or provide money for a cost or expense
    
    🧠 defray = de-fee = pay
    
    **The scholarship helped defray the cost of tuition.
    → It helped pay for tuition.**
    
    **The company will defray travel expenses for employees.
    → The company will cover travel costs.**
    
    **Donations were used to defray the expenses of the event.
    → Donations paid for the event costs.**

- **zeitgeist**
    
    *noun*
    
    the defining spirit, mood, or ideas of a particular time period
    
    🧠 zeitgist = zeit(time)+geist(spirit) = spirit of a time
    
    **Social media has become a major part of the modern zeitgeist.
    → It represents the spirit of the current era.**
    
    **The artist captured the zeitgeist of the 1960s.
    → The artist captured the ideas and mood of that time.**
    
    **Environmental awareness is part of today's cultural zeitgeist.
    → It reflects the current cultural atmosphere.**
    
- **benighted**
    
    *adjective*
    
    ignorant, lacking knowledge or understanding, or living in an unenlightened condition ⇒ dimag bodho state ma hunu
    
    Core Idea: you’re not in a bright spot
    
    🧠 benighted = be+night = not in enlightened state
    
    **The reformer criticized the benighted attitudes of the ruling elite.
    → The attitudes were ignorant and unenlightened.**
    
    **The novel portrays a benighted society resistant to education.
    → A society lacking knowledge and progress.**
    
    **He remained benighted about basic scientific principles.
    → He was ignorant of them.**

- **factitious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH factious
    
    artificial; not natural; made up or manufactured; not genuine
    
    syn: **artificial; fake; simulated; manufactured**
    
    🧠 factitious = fake+tious = not genuine
    
    🧠 factitious = fact+tedious = fact+iss serious(not serious) = opposite of facts = fake
    
    **The journalist exposed the factitious controversy created for publicity.
    → The controversy was artificially created.**
    
    **The company tried to create factitious demand for its product.
    → The demand was manufactured, not genuine.**
    
    **The argument was based on a factitious distinction.
    → The distinction was artificially invented.**
