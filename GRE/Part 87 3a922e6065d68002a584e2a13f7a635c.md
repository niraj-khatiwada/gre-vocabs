- **surly**
    
    *adjective*
    
     bad-tempered, unfriendly, rude, or unpleasant in manner.
    
    🧠 surly = sir(teacher)+ly = grumpy 😡
    
    🧠 surly = sourly = angry
    
    **The waiter gave a surly response when asked for help.
    → The waiter was rude and unfriendly.**
    
    **The old man was known for his surly personality.
    → He was often grumpy and unpleasant.**
    
    **The dog looked surly and aggressive.
    → The dog appeared angry and unfriendly.**

- **rankle**
    
    *verb*
    
    irritate, annoy, anger, or cause persistent resentment.
    
    syn: **vex**
    
    🧠 rankle = rank + anger = you resent on years of unjust ranking

    **The issue still rankles among employees.

- **precedent**
    
    *noun*
    
    an example that is used to justify similar occurrences at a later time
    
    🧠 precedent = we know unprecedented means never happened/existed before = so precedented means already happened = so precedent means we use that already happened event as an example later in time
    
    🧠 precedent = derived from precede = to happen before in time
    
- **egotist** vs **egoist**
    
    *noun*
    
    egotist = talks about oneself excessively
    
    🧠 ego**t**ist = talkative 
    
    ---
    
    egoist = thinks oneself is more important than others (does not talks but acts like it)
    
    🧠 egoist = too much ego
    
    **Egotist = "I am great!" 🗣️ (self-talk)**
    
    **Egoist = "I come first." 🥇 (self-interest)**
    
- **hamstrung**
    
    *adjective*
    
    prevented from functioning effectively; severely limited or hindered ⇒ powerless
    
    syn: **hindered; hampered; impaired**
    
    🧠 hamstrung = too much hamstring 🦵 exercise hindered your leg to walk = prevented from functioning effectively
    
    **The company was hamstrung by a lack of funding.
    → The company was severely limited because it lacked money.**
    
    **The team was hamstrung by injuries.
    → Injuries prevented them from performing well.**
    
    **The new regulations hamstrung innovation.
    → The regulations restricted progress.**
