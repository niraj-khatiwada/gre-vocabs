# Part 36

- **exalt**
    
    *verb*
    
    praise or glorify
    
    syn: **lionize; venerate**
    
    🧠 **exalt** = extra altitude = raise someone’s power, status = praise
    
- **lionize**
    
    *verb*
    
    treat someone as celebrity or hero or king (lion) = glorify
    
    syn: **venerate; revere; exalt; deify**
    
    🧠 lionize = lion 🦁 = treat someone like lion (king) = glorify
    
- **extol**
    
    *verb*
    
    to praise someone or something very highly and enthusiastically
    
    🧠 extol = extra+tall = make someone extra tall by praising enthusiastically
    
    **Although critics initially dismissed the novel, they later extolled it as a masterpiece.**
    
    ---
    
    *noun*
    
    extolment
    
- **monotonous**
    
    *adjective*
    
    something that is very boring or dull because it’s always same and has no variation
    
    syn: **tedious; banal; humdrum; wearying**
    
    🧠 monotonous = mono + tedious
    
    ---
    
    *noun*
    
    monotony
    
- **premeditate**
    
    *verb*
    
    plan before doing something
    
    syn: **conspire; scheme; orchestrate**
    
    🧠 premeditate = pre + meditate = meditate (think) before
    
    **The theft was clearly premeditated.**
    
- **relinquish**
    
    *verb*
    
    to give up a possession, claim or control
    
    syn: **renounce; cede; forsake**
    
    🧠 relinquish = release + quit = you give up and release the possession
    
    **Iran has refused to relinquish control over the Strait of Hormuz.**
    
- **synoptic**
    
    *noun*
    
    providing a summary or general overview
    
    syn: **condensed; summarized**
    
    🧠 synoptic = derived from synopsis which means a general summary of something large
    
- **skittish**
    
    *adjective*
    
    timid; easily scared; frightened; jumpy
    
    syn: **restive; fidgety; anxious**
    
    🧠 skittish = skittish kitten
