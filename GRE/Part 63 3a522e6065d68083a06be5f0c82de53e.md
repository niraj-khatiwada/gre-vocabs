# Part 63

- **intrigue**
    
    *noun/verb*
    
    Meaning 1(noun):  A secret plan, plot, or scheme (usually of bad intent)
    
    Meaning 2(verb): To interest, fascinate, or arouse curiosity 
    
    syn: **conspiracy; scheme**
    
- **machinate**
    
    *verb*
    
    to secretly plan or scheme, especially for a harmful or selfish purpose.
    
    syn: **intrigue**
    
    🧠 machinate = machines create a secret plan to harm avengers
    
    **The villains machinated to overthrow the king.
    → They secretly planned a scheme.**
    
    **He was accused of machinating against his rivals.
    → He secretly plotted against them.**
    
    **The politicians were machinating behind the scenes.
    → They were making secret plans.**

- **nominal**
    
    *adjective*
    
    Meaning 1: Very small; insignificant; much less than expected
    
    Nominal = tiny amount, almost just in name.
    
    Example:
    
    - ***The organization charges a nominal fee of $1.***
    - ***He received only a nominal salary for his work.***
    
    Meaning 2: Existing in name only, not in reality
    
    Nominal = officially called something, but not truly functioning that way.
    
    Example:
    
    - ***The king was the nominal leader, but others controlled the government.***
