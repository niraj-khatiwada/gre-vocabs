# Part 84

- **immaterial**
    
    *adjective*
    
    not relevant; not important ⇒ does not matter
    
    syn: **irrelevant; insignificant; trivial; negligible; inconsequential**
    
    🧠 immaterial = im+material(material means something that matters) = does not matter
    
    🧠 immaterial = im+matter = does not matter
    
- **rail**
    
    *verb*
    
    to complain angrily or criticize strongly ⇒ condemn with harsh words
    
    syn: **berate; condemn**
    
    **The critics railed against the new policy.
    → They strongly criticized it.**
    
    **He spent hours railing about government corruption.
    → He complained angrily.**
    
- **becoming**
    
    *adjective*
    
     appropriate, and matches nicely; suits nicely
    
    syn: **fitting; apt**
    
    🧠 becoming
    
    Becoming = Be + Coming to you
    
    Imagine a style or behavior that "comes" naturally to someone.
    
    👔 A suit fits a businessman.
    
    👑 Confidence fits a leader.
    
    It is something that belongs with that person.
    
    Becoming = fitting, suitable, flattering
    
    **Her dress was becoming and made her look even more beautiful.**
    
- **replete**
    
    *verb*
    
    completely stocked or furnished with something ⇒ to completely fill with something
    
    syn: **packed; loaded; filled; full**
    
    🧠 replete = re + plate = re-stock 
    
    **The museum is replete with ancient artifacts.
    → The museum is full of ancient artifacts.**
    
    **The book is replete with useful examples.
    → The book contains many useful examples.**
    
    **The garden was replete with colorful flowers.
    → The garden was overflowing with flowers.**
    
- **start**
    
    *verb*
    
    to suddenly move or react because of surprise ⇒ jolt of flinch due to surprise
    
    syn: **startle; flinch; jump**
    
    🧠 start = startle = jump due to surprise
    
- **startle**
    
    *verb*
    
    syn: **start**
    
    ***Something → startles → you***
    
    ***You → start → because of something***
    
    **The thunder startled the sleeping baby. ⇒ thunder startled**
    
    **The baby was started by the thunder ⇒ baby started**
