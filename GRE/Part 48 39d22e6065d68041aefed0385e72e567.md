# Part 48

- **implicit**
    
    *adjective*
    
    suggested; not directly expressed
    
    syn: **implied; insinuated**
    
    🧠 implicit =implied
    
- **inviolate**
    
    *adjective*
    
    free from harm or violation
    
    syn: **pristine; sacred; sacrosanct**
    
    🧠 inviolate = no violation
    
- **profane**
    
    *verb*
    
    to treat with disrespect or vulgar language
    
    🧠 profane = profanity
    
- **liability**
    
    *noun*
    
    something that holds you back
    
    syn: **burden; nuisance**
    
    🧠 liability = debt
    
- **prodigious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    enormously large in size or amount or quality
    
    syn: **colossal; enormous; tremendous**
    
    🧠 prodigious = prodigy = enormous talent
    
- **qualm**
    
    *noun*
    
    a feeling of doubt or apprehension or worry or hesitation
    
    syn: **reservation; hesitation; misgiving; uneasiness**
    
    🧠 qualm = question about calm = apprehension
    
- **stint**
    
    *verb*
    
    Meaning 1: limit or restrict resources
    
    syn: **spare; tightfisted; frugal**
    
    🧠 stint = stingy
    
    **Don't stint on the food ⇒ don’t be stingy on buying/eating food**
    
    Meaning 2:  a fixed period of time spent on a job or activity, an assigned task
    
    **Despite his short stint as a waiter, he learned the importance of hard work and good service.** 
    
- **temper**
    
    *verb*
    
    to soften or moderate
    
    syn: **mitigate; alleviate; ameliorate**
    
- **vivacious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    full of life and joy
    
    syn: **ebullient; buoyant; bubbly**
    
- **unconscionable**
    
    *adjective*
    
    something that cannot be done on  good conscience
    
    Conscience is an inner moral compass that guides you to distinguish between right and wrong.
