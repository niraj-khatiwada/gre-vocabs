# Part 109

- **unstinting**
    
    *adjective*
    
    very generous; giving freely without holding back ⇒ not tightfisted or frugal
    
    🧠 unstinting  = opposite of stinting
    
    **She gave unstinting support to her friends.
    → She gave complete, generous support.**
    
    **The volunteers received unstinting praise for their work.
    → They received abundant praise.**
    
    **His unstinting efforts helped the project succeed.
    → He put in enormous effort without restraint.**
    
- **ineffable**
    
    *adjective*
    
    too great, beautiful, sacred, or intense to be expressed in words.
    
    Core idea: Beyond words
    
    🧠 ineffable = in+effectable = unable to express in words
    
    **She felt ineffable joy when she saw her child.
    → Joy so great it could not be described.**
    
    **The beauty of the mountain landscape was ineffable.
    → Too magnificent for words.**
    
    **The poem tries to capture an ineffable feeling.
    → A feeling that cannot be expressed.**
    
- **trammel**
    
    *noun/verb*
    
    to restrict, confine, hinder, or prevent free movement or action.
    
    Core Idea: hold back
    
    🧠 trammel = trammel nets = restrict or confine
    
    **The new regulations trammeled innovation.
    → They restricted innovation.**
    
    **His fear trammeled his ambition.
    → His fear held him back.**
    
    **She refused to let social expectations trammel her choices.
    → She refused to let them limit her.**

- **epigram**
    
    *noun*
    
    a short, clever, and witty saying
    
    Core idea: A sharp, clever one-liner.
    
    🧠 epigram = epic+telegram(short message) = a sharp message
    
    **My favorite epigram from Mark Twain is "A man who carries a cat by the tail learns something he can learn no other way."**
    
- **bristle**
    
    *verb*
    
    react in an offended or angry manner
    
    🧠 bristle = be+riled = angry😠
    
    **She bristled at the accusation.
    → She became angry or offended.**
    
    **He bristled when his work was criticized.
    → He reacted defensively.**
    
    **The dog bristled when it sensed danger.
    → Its hairs stood up because of aggression/fear.**

- **percipient**
    
    *adjective*
    
    having a keen ability to notice, understand, or perceive things. ⇒ highly perceptive
    
    syn: **observant; insightful; shrewd**
    
    🧠 percipient = perceive+recipient = observant 
    
    **Even the most percipient editor will make an occasional error when proofreading.**
    
- **rapprochement**
    
    *noun*
    
    the restoration of friendly relations between people, groups, or countries after a period of conflict or disagreement.
    
    syn: **reconciliation**
    
    🧠 rapprochement = re+approach+agreement 🤝 = reconciliation
    
    **The two countries worked toward rapprochement after decades of hostility.
    → They tried to rebuild friendly relations.**
    
    **The meeting marked a rapprochement between the rival companies.
    → They became closer after conflict.**
    
    **Their conversation led to a gradual rapprochement.
    → Their relationship improved.**
    
- **deign**
    
    *verb*
    
    to do something reluctantly because you think it is below your dignity
    
    Core Idea: I’m too important for this, but I’ll do it anyway.
    
    🧠 deign = de(below)+dignity👑 = do something below your dignity
    
    **The celebrity deigned to speak to the reporters.
    → She reluctantly or condescendingly spoke to them.**
    
    **He would not deign to apologize.
    → He felt apologizing was beneath him.**
    
- **ersatz**
    
    *adjective*
    
    not real or genuine; phony; counterfeit
    
    🧠 ersatz = artificial substitute 🎭
    
    **The restaurant served ersatz coffee made from roasted grains.
    → A substitute for real coffee.**
    
    **The movie felt like an ersatz version of the original classic.
    → An inferior imitation.**
    
    **During the shortage, people relied on ersatz materials.
    → Replacement materials.**
