- **substantial**
    
    *adjective*
    
    large in size, amount or importance
    
    **The company made a substantial profit this year.
    → A large profit.**
    
    **She has substantial experience in software engineering.
    → A lot of valuable experience.**
    
    **The evidence provided a substantial reason for concern.
    → A strong/significant reason.**
    
- **substantiate**
    
    *verb*
    
    to provide evidence to support; to prove something by providing evidence
    
    🧠 substantiate = provide substance as evidence or provide substantial information as an evidence
    
    **The scientist could not substantiate the theory.
    → Could not prove it with evidence.**
    
    **The lawyer provided documents to substantiate the accusation.
    → Provided evidence to support it.**
    
    **The company must substantiate its claims with data.
    → Must back up claims with proof.**
