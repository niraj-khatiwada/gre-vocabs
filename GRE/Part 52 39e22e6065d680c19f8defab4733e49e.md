    🧠 archetype = imagine archaic buildings = standard

- **bereft**
    
    *adjective*
    
    deprived of something important, especially through loss; often left lonely or empty after losing someone or something.
    
    syn: **deprived; wanting; forlorn**
    
    🧠 bereft = be + left = you’re left lonely after losing someone

- **forlorn**
    
    *adjective*
    
    lonely, abandoned, or hopeless.
    
    syn: **desolate; dejected**
    
    🧠 forlorn = forgotten + alone = forever+lone
    
- **desolate**
    
    *adjective*
    
    Physical Meaning:  a place that is uninhabited, barren, and empty of life or people
    
    Core Idea: isolated, sad and lonely
    
    syn: **bereft; forlorn**
    
    🧠 desolate = desert+isolate = lonely
    
- **captious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    finding fault over small, trivial matters; overly critical, especially in a petty or nitpicky way.
    
    syn: **faultfinding, critical, hypercritical, nitpicky, censorious, carping, peevish, querulous, picky**
    
    🧠 **capt**ious = captain always nitpicking in the plane trying to find faults

- **carping**
    
    *adjective*
    
    constant complaining
    
    syn: **captious; nitpicky**
    
    🧠 carping = car + park = an old man in car park always complaining
    
- **conclusive**
    
    *adjective*
    
    without doubt; true; ⇒ 100%
    
    syn: **incontrovertible; irrefutable; decisive**
    
    🧠 conclusive = conclusion = 100%
    
    **The police found conclusive evidence that solved the case.**
    
    **We need conclusive proof before we make a final choice**
    
- **disentangle**

    syn: **liberate; extract**

    *verb*
