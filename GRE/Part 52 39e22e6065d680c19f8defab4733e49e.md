# Part 52

- **appropriate**
    
    *verb*
    
    to take something by force
    
    syn: **seize**
    
    **cultural appropriation ⇒ seizing culture**
    
- **archetype**
    
    *noun*
    
    something shown as an example or model or standard
    
    syn: **prototype; representative; standard; paradigm**
    
    🧠 archetype = imagine archaic buildings = standard
    
    ---
    
    *adjective*
    
    archetypal
    
- **auspice**
    
    *noun*
    
    auspicious or favorable
    
    🧠 auspice = noun version of adjective auspicious
    
- **empirically**
    
    *adverb*
    
    based on calculations, and real world data instead of just being a theory
    
    🧠 empirically = empire of science 
    
- **bereft**
    
    *adjective*
    
    deprived of something important, especially through loss; often left lonely or empty after losing someone or something.
    
    syn: **deprived; wanting; forlorn**
    
    🧠 bereft = be + left = you’re left lonely after losing someone
    
    ![image.png](Part%2052/image.png)
    
- **forlorn**
    
    *adjective*
    
    lonely, abandoned, or hopeless.
    
    syn: **desolate; dejected**
    
    🧠 forlorn = forgotten + alone = forever+lone
    
- **desolate**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **bereft; forlorn**
    
- **captious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    finding fault over small, trivial matters; overly critical, especially in a petty or nitpicky way.
    
    syn: **faultfinding, critical, hypercritical, nitpicky, censorious, carping, peevish, querulous, picky**
    
    🧠 **capt**ious = captain always nitpicking in the plane trying to find faults
    
    ![image.png](Part%2052/image%201.png)
    
- **carping**
    
    *adjective*
    
    constant complaining
    
    syn: **captious; nitpicky**
    
    🧠 carping = car + park = an old man in car park always complaining
    
- **conclusive**
    
    *adjective*
    
    without doubt; true; ⇒ 100%
    
    syn: **incontrovertible; irrefutable; decisive**
    
    🧠 conclusive = conclusion
    
- **disentangle**
    
    *verb*
    
    make clear; free from confusion or complex topic
    
    syn: **liberate; extract**
    
    🧠 disentangle = dis + entangle
    
- **exhort**
    
    *verb*
    
    strongly persuade, encourage or urge
    
    syn: **adjure**
    
    🧠 exhort = ex + hurt = pressure your ex to get back 😜
    
    🧠 exhort = assert = force
    
- **adjure**
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH abjure
    
    *verb*
    
    syn: **exhort**
    
    a**b**jure = to renounce or criticize harshly
    
    a**d**jure = pressure or force someone to do something
    
- **frailty**
    
    *noun*
    
    state of being weak or faltered
    
    syn: **feebleness; fragility**
    
    🧠 frailty = feeble; falter; fragile
    
    ---
    
    *adjective*
    
    frail
    
    **As we get older, we’ll become frail.**
