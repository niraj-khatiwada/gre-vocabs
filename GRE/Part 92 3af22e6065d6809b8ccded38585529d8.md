- **behoove**
    
    *verb*
    
    it is a duty, responsibility, or wise choice for someone to do something ⇒ if you do this, you’ll be responsible and it will benefit you
    
    🧠 behoove = be+hoove(help you) = be helpful
    
    **It behooves you to study regularly before the exam.
    → It is wise/advantageous for you to study.**
    
    **It would behoove the company to improve customer service.
    → It would be beneficial for the company.**

- **grudge**
    
    *noun*
    
    a strong, lasting feeling of anger, bitterness, or resentment toward someone because of a past wrongdoing
    
- **begrudge**
    
    *verb*
    
    Meaning 1: to envy someone for enjoying something ⇒ imagine you have a grudge with someone and that person enjoys, you envy on that
    
    **He begrudged his colleague's success.
    → He resented his colleague for being successful.**
    
    **She did not begrudge him his happiness.
    → She did not resent him for being happy.**
    
    Meaning 2: to give or allow something reluctantly ⇒ due to your grudge, you have a hesitation towards something
    
    **The manager begrudged the employees their extra vacation days.
    → The manager disliked giving them those benefits.**
