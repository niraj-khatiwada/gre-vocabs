- **derelict**
    
    *noun/adjective*
    
    Meaning 1: abandoned or neglected
    
    **The old factory has become derelict. → It is abandoned and falling apart.**
    
    **The police found a derelict ship floating near the coast. → An abandoned ship.**
    
    Meaning 2: (of a person) not doing one's duties ⇒ abandon your duties
    
    **The teacher was derelict in her duties because she hadn't graded a single student paper in three weeks.**
    
    🧠 derelict = de+released = game was not released = abandoned

- **hagiographic**
    
    *adjective*
    
    overly praising or idealizing someone, especially by portraying them as perfect or saint-like.
    
    Core Idea: portraying someone as too positive.
    
    🧠 hagiographic = holy+biography = portraying someone as holy/saint/pure only
    
    **Most accounts of Tiger Woods's life were hagiographic, until, that is, his affairs made headlines.**
    
    **The biography was criticized as hagiographic because it ignored the leader's mistakes.
    → It portrayed him too positively.**
    
    **The documentary had a hagiographic tone toward the celebrity.
    → It praised the celebrity excessively.**
    
- **grandiloquent**
    
    *adjective*
    
    using grand, impressive-sounding language that is often exaggerated, pompous, or pretentious.
    
    Core Idea: big words, little significance
    
    🧠 grandiloquent = grand+loquacious
    
    **The politician gave a grandiloquent speech full of promises but few details.
    → The language sounded impressive but was exaggerated.**
    
    **His grandiloquent writing made simple ideas seem complicated.
    → He used overly fancy language.**
    
    **The proposal was filled with grandiloquent phrases about "changing humanity."
    → Dramatic, inflated wording.**

- **nonplussed**
    
    *adjective*
    
    surprised, confused, or unsure how to react.
    
    syn: **flummoxed**; **bewildered; baffled; perplexed; bemused**
    
    🧠 nonplussed
    
    **"Nonplussed = No + Plus"**
    
    Imagine:
    
    You are solving a math problem and suddenly there is **no plus (+) sign anywhere**.
    
    Your brain freezes:
    
    "What do I do now?"
    
    You are confused and unable to react. ****⇒ You are **nonplussed.**

- **quail**
    
    *verb*
    
    to withdraw or retreat due to fear and apprehension
    
    🧠 quail = quiet+aside = quietly withdraw
    
    🧠 quail = quail birds go back to nest on danger(fear)
    
    **She quailed at the thought of speaking in front of thousands of people.
    → She became afraid.**
    
    **The soldiers did not quail in the face of danger.
    → They did not lose courage.**
    
    **He quailed before his opponent's confidence.
    → He felt intimidated.**

- **lugubrious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    extremely sad, gloomy, or mournful.
    
    🧠 lugubrious = lull(quiet)+gloomy(sad) = sad and gloomy
    
    **The funeral had a lugubrious atmosphere.
    → The atmosphere was very sad and mournful.**
    
    **He spoke in a lugubrious voice about his failures.
    → His voice sounded gloomy and sorrowful.**
    
    **The movie's lugubrious tone made it feel depressing.
    → The mood was dark and sad.**
    
- **umbrage**
    
    *adjective*
    
    a feeling of anger or annoyance; a feeling of anger caused by being offended
    
    syn: **insult; resentment; indignation; grievance**
    
    🧠 umbrage = umbrella + rage = you resent under the umbrella contemplating in the rain
    
- **enjoin**
    
    *verb*
    
    to instruct, order, or command someone to do something by authority
    
    🧠 enjoin = enemy+join = you order a missile attack when enemy joins the land
    
    **The judge enjoined the company to stop using the trademark.
    → The judge ordered them to stop.**
    
    **The teacher enjoined students to work hard.
    → The teacher urged/instructed students.**
    
    **The court enjoined the publication of the document.
    → The court prohibited its publication.**
