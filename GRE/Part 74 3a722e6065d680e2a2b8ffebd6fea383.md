# Part 74

- **apocryphal**
    
    *adjective*
    
    of doubtful authenticity; a story or claim that is widely believed but probably not true.
    
    syn: **dubious, fictitious, unauthenticated; spurious; specious**
    
    🧠 apocryphal = a cry for fake = not authentic
    
    **The story that Newton discovered gravity after an apple fell on his head is often considered apocryphal.**
    
    **The internet is full of apocryphal tales about famous people.**
    
- **spurious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **apocryphal**
    
- **specious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **spurious**
    
- **espouse**
    
    *verb*
    
    to adopt or support an idea or cause
    
    syn: **advocate; endorse; embrace**
    
    🧠 espouse = embrace spouse = support spouse
    
- **exalt**
    
    *verb*
    
    praise or glorify; to raise someone in rank, power, or status
    
    syn: **lionize; venerate**
    
    🧠 **exalt** = extra altitude = raise someone’s power, status = praise
    
- **lionize**
    
    *verb*
    
    treat someone as celebrity or hero or king (lion)
    
    syn: **venerate; revere; exalt; deify**
    
    🧠 lionize = lion 🦁 = treat someone like a lion (king)
    
- **expound**
    
    *verb*
    
    to explain something in detail and clearly.
    
    syn: **expatiate; embellish**
    
    🧠 expound = explain + expand
    
    **She spent an hour expounding her theory about human behavior.**
    
- **expatiate**
    
    *verb*
    
    to speak or write in great detail about a topic; to elaborate or discuss at length.
    
    syn: **expound; embellish**
    
    🧠 expatiate = expand(explain) + elaborate

- **blatant**
    
    *adjective*
    
    done openly and unashamedly.
    
    **“blatant lies”**
    
    **“blatant comments”**
    
    ---
    
    very obvious; without trying to hide.
    
    **“Notwithstanding their blatant relationship, they still claim they are friends.”**
    
- **awry**
    
    *adverb*
    
    goes wrong, off course, out of order, or not going as planned.
    
    🧠 awry = a wrong way
    
    **AI bet goes awry, Oracle fires 21000 employees.**
    
- **dilettante**
    
    *noun*
    
    an amateur who dabbles without deep expertise.
    
    syn: **layman; amateur; dabbler; hobbyist**
    
    🧠 dilettante = de + literate = having little expertise or knowledge in a field
    
    🧠 dilettante = do little = an amateur
    
    **Fred has no formal medical training; while he likes to claim authority on medical issues, he is little more than a dilettant**
