# Part 113

- **canard**
    
    *noun*
    
    a false story or misleading claim that is presented as if it were true
    
    Core Idea: a deliberately misleading fabrication
    
    🧠 canard = con+art = misleading art = a false story = fabricated story
    
    **The claim that vaccines contain tracking devices is a canard.
    → A false rumor.**
    
    **The politician dismissed the accusation as a canard.
    → A baseless claim.**
    
    **The idea that success comes without hard work is a common canard.
    → A misleading belief.**

    **The fake painting was a simulacrum of the original masterpiece.
