- **incisive**
    
    *adjective*
    
    sharp, clear, and able to analyze or understand something quickly and accurately.
    
    syn: **astute; shrewd; sharp**
    
    🧠 incisive = insightful + precise
    
    - **The journalist asked incisive questions that exposed the truth.
    → sharp, insightful questions**
    - **Her incisive analysis revealed the weaknesses in the argument.
    → clear and intelligent analysis**
