# Part 24

- **myopic**
    
    *adjective*
    
    short-sighted; lacking creativity and intellectual
    
    syn: **insular; parochial; narrow-minded; provincial**
    
    🧠 myopic = derived from myopia = shortsightedness | hyperopia = farsightedness
    
- **pensive**
    
    *adjective*
    
    deep in thought; contemplate for a long time
    
    syn: **contemplative; reflective; introspective**
    
    🧠 **pen**sive = pen + thinking = deep thought
    
    ![image.png](Part%2024/image.png)
    
- **portend**
    
    *verb*
    
    To be a sign or warning that something (usually bad) is going to happen
    
    Core Idea: foreshadow or hint something negative will happen in the future
    
    syn: **foreshadow; foretell; augur**
    
    🧠 portend = port + end = something bad happening on port’s end = something bad will happen on strait of hormuz
    
- **foreshadow**
    
    *verb*
    
    hint about something that’ll happen later
    
    syn: **portend; fortell**
    
- **augur**
    
    *verb*
    
    syn: **portend; foreshadow**
    
- **slight**
    
    *verb*
    
    treat with disrespect; ignore; neglect as not important
    
    syn: **snub; disregard**
    
- **tout**
    
    *verb*
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH taunt
    
    promote or praise something excessively often in a sales-like way 📢
    
    syn: **promote; advertise; endorse; hype; publicize**
    
    🧠 tout = shout 📢
    
- **heyday**
    
    *adjective*
    
    period of great popularity, prosperity
    
    syn: **pinnacle; culminating; bloom; boom; prime; apex; zenith**
    
    🧠 **he**y**day** = height + day = peak day = peak period
    
- **zenith**
    
    *adjective*
    
    highest point of something
    
    🧠 zenith = it literally means highest point of something

- **levity**
    
    *noun*
    
    lacking seriousness (usually in serious situations like funeral)
    
    syn: **frivolity; flippancy; lighthearted**
    
- **frivolity**
    
    *noun*
    
    syn: **levity**
    
- **flippancy**
    
    *noun*
    
    syn: **levity; frivolity**
