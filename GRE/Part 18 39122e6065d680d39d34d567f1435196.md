- **reprimand**
    
    *verb*
    
    > Reprimand is more related to condemnation but it’s meaning is similar to castigate which is harsh/strong form of reprimand.
    > 
- **chide**
    
    *verb*
    
    > Same as reprimand but this is the mildest form of criticizing
    > 
- **rebuke**
    
    *verb*
    
    public form of disapproval; (mildest just like chide)
    
    syn: **reprimand**, condemn, criticize, censure
    
    > Calm and firm disapproval without rage.
    > 
    
    Summary
    
    - castigate; pillory= Harshest
    - reprimand; censure = milder (on stronger side)
    - chide; rebuke = mildest
