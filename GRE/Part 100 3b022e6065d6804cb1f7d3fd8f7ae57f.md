- **arriviste**

    syn: **parvenu; upstart; amateur; novice; newcomer**
    
    🧠 arriviste = arrive+status = you reach the higher status and start to show off = **“I’ve arrived fellas”**🏎️
    
    **The wealthy arriviste tried to impress everyone with expensive clothes and cars.
    → A newly successful person showing off.**
    
    **Old aristocratic families looked down on the arriviste businessman.
    → They saw him as a social climber.**
    
    **The party was full of arrivistes eager to display their new wealth.
    → Newly rich people seeking status.**
