- **tempered**
    
    *adjective*
    
    to moderate or balance; soften; not extreme
    
    🧠 tempered = remember temperance? yes, it’s similar to that = to make something balanced
    
    Usually used as `-tempered` phrases:
    
    - high-tempered = high emotions
    - soft-tempered = soft emotions
    - quick-tempered = quick emotions
- **constituent**
    
    *noun/adjective*
    
    Meaning 1: Part of something
    
    **Water's constituents are hydrogen and oxygen.**
    → Hydrogen and oxygen are parts that make up water.
    
    Meaning 2: Voter/member (politics)
    
    **The senator met with her constituents to discuss local issues.**
    → The people she represents.
