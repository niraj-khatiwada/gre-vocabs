- **eponym**
    
    *noun*
    
    a name derived from a person (real or imaginary), or the person for whom something is named
    
    🧠 eponym = epic+name = named after epic people
    
    **The newton is an eponym derived from Isaac Newton's name and is used as the SI unit of force.**
    
    **The sandwich became an eponym when the Earl of Sandwich's name was adopted for the food he popularized.**
    
    **The diesel engine is an eponym named after Rudolf Diesel, the engineer who developed it.**
    
    **The boycott was an eponym derived from Charles Boycott, whose name became associated with organized refusal to deal with someone.**

- **surfeit**
    
    *noun/verb*

    syn: **surplus; abundance; plethora**

    🧠 surfeit = surplus + feast = too much
    
- **concomitant**
    
    *adjective*
    
    describing an event or situation that happens at the same time as or in connection with another
    
    syn: **concurrent; correlated**
    
    🧠 concomitant = co-companion = happening together
    
    **Fever is often concomitant with infection.
    → Fever accompanies infection.**
    
    **A broken window is a common concomitant of burglary.
    → The broken window often accompanies a burglary.**
    
    **Police found several concomitant signs of burglary: forced entry, missing valuables, and disturbed rooms.
    → These signs came along with the crime.**
    
    **The neighborhood experienced a rise in burglaries, with a concomitant increase in security camera installations.
    → More burglaries happened, and at the same time security measures increased.**

- **exiguity**
    
    *noun*
    
    extreme scarcity or insufficiency of amount, size, or resources ⇒ a state of meager resources
    
    ant: **surfeit**
