- **distend**
    
    *verb*
    
    to swell, expand, or stretch out because of pressure or being filled.
    
    Core Idea: become larger by being stretched from inside.
    
    syn: **bulge; dilate (**dilated eyes**); bloat**
    
    🧠 distend = dilate+ swell + extend
    
    **The medicine caused my stomach to distend, making me feel like I had swallowed a giant beach ball.**

- **shriveled**
    
    *adjective*
    
    dried up, wrinkled, shrunken, or reduced in size because of loss of moisture or vitality.
    
    🧠 shriveled = shrink + wrinkled 🍎
    
    **The shriveled apple had been left out for weeks.
    → It became dry and wrinkled.**
    
    **The plant became shriveled without water.
    → It dried up and shrank.**
    
    **His hopes were shriveled by repeated failures.
    → His hopes were weakened/reduced.**
    
- **providential**
    
    *adjective*
    
    Meaning 1: apt; well-suited 
    
    syn: **felicitous**
    
    Meaning 2: happening at a fortunate time; seemingly caused by divine guidance or luck ⇒ well-suited that’s why happened at right time
    
    syn: **auspicious**
    
    🧠 providential = provide+destiny = lucky
    
    🧠 providential = providence (a divine or guidance) created it = lucky
    
    **His discovery of the lost document was providential.
    → It happened at exactly the right time.**
    
    **A providential rain saved the crops from drought.
    → A fortunate, timely event.**
    
    **Meeting my future business partner was a providential encounter.
    → A lucky meeting that seemed destined.**
