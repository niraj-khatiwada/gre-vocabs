- **milieu**
    
    *noun*
    
    the social environment, surroundings, or setting in which someone or something exists.
    
    🧠 milieu = my+life+environment = my life in EU = surroundings where i live
    
    **She grew up in a scientific milieu.
    → She was surrounded by scientists and scientific culture.**
    
    **The novel captures the artistic milieu of Paris in the 1920s.

    **His ideas were shaped by his political milieu.
    → His surroundings and social environment influenced him.**
    
- **descry**
    
    *verb*
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH decry
    
    to catch sight of, discover, or detect something that is difficult to see or notice.
    
    Core Idea: spot something hidden or far away.
    
    🧠 descry = detect+spy 👀 = detective+spot 👀 = spot something that is difficult to notice
    
    🧠 descry = discover with your eye 👀  = caught sight of
    
    **From the hilltop, they descried a ship on the horizon.
    → They spotted it.**
    
    **The detective descried a clue hidden in the document.
    → He noticed/discovered it.**
    
    **She descried a faint light in the distance.
    → She caught sight of it.**

- **palliate**
    
    *verb*

    > 
    
    🧠 palliate = pain+alleviate = make less severe
    
    🧠 palliate = partial+alleviate = make severe partially

    **The government introduced temporary measures to palliate the crisis.
