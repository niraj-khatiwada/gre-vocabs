- **winnow**
    
    *verb*
    
    to separate or remove unwanted parts from something, leaving only what is useful or valuable. ⇒  filter out the bad, keep the good.
    
    syn: **filter; sort; screen; eliminate**
    
    🧠 winnow
    
    Imagine a competition:
    
    Many people enter.
    
    You remove weaker competitors until only the best person can **win now**.
    
    **Winnow = remove the unnecessary to find the winner.**
    
    **The editor winnowed hundreds of applications down to the best candidates.
    → The editor removed weaker candidates and kept the strongest ones.**
    
    **The investigation helped winnow the possible causes of the problem.
    → It narrowed down the possibilities.**
    
    **Farmers winnow grain to separate the useful seeds from the chaff.
    → They remove unwanted material.**
