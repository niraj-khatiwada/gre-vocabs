# Part 39

- **abet**
    
    *verb*
    
    to help, encourage, or support someone in doing something (usually something wrong or illegal).
    
    syn: **aid; assist; encourage; back**
    
    🧠 abet = a bet  to do something illegal
    
    🧠 abet = usually used in crime
    
    **Police abetted thief during the heist.**
    
- **accessible**
    
    *adjective*
    
    capable of understanding; clear
    
    syn: **comprehensible; penetrable; fathomable**
    
    🧠 accessible = is a common word for something that is easily found but in terms of GRE it means something that is easily understandable
    
- **attenuate**
    
    *verb*
    
    weaken or lessen in force, intensity or amount
    
    syn: **diminish; dampen; erode**
    
    🧠 attenuate = a tiny = make smaller
    
    ---
    
    *noun*
    
    attenuation
    
- **augmentation**
    
    *noun*
    
    to make something bigger
    
    🧠 augmentation = derived from verb augment
    
- **averse**
    
    *adjective*
    
    extreme dislike or hatred
    
    syn: abhorrence; loathing; antipathy
    
    🧠 averse = derived from noun aversion
    
- **blithe**
    
    *adjective*
    
    carefree; lacking concern or seriousness
    
    syn: **heedless; nonchalant**
    
    🧠 blithe = b + lit = be lit = don’t give a f**k
    
- **dawdle**
    
    *verb*
    
    move slowly and without purpose; delaying; to waste time
    
    syn: **delay, linger, procrastinate, dally, dilly-dally**
    
    🧠 dawdle = delay = move slowly
    
- **dally**
    
    *verb*
    
    syn: **dawdle**
    
- **discount**
    
    *verb*
    
    to make something trivial
    
    syn: **ignore; disregard; snub; overlook**
    
- **dissident**
    
    *noun/adjective*
    
    someone who disagrees with official policies or beliefs; a rebel against authority
    
    syn: **rebel; nonconformist; renegade**
    
    🧠 dissident = dis + obedient = not obeying authorities
