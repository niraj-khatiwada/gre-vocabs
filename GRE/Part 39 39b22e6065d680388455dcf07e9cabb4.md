- **abet**
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH abate
    
    *verb*
    
    to help, encourage, or support someone in doing something (usually something wrong or illegal).
    
    syn: **aid; assist; encourage; back**
    
    🧠 abet = a bet(help) to do something illegal =  ***Would you help me? You bet!***
    
    🧠 abet = usually used in crime
    
    **Police abetted thief during the heist.**
    
- **abate**
    
    *verb*
    
    to become less strong or to reduce in intensity, amount, or value
    
    syn: diminish; **wane;** lessen
    
    🧠 abate = a bit less
