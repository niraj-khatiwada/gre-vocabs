# Part 80

- **peruse**
    
    *verb*
    
    to examine or read something carefully and thoroughly.
    
    syn: **examine; inspect; study; scrutinize; review; analyze**
    
    🧠 peruse = pure use of eyes 👀 = read carefully
    
    **She perused the research paper before the meeting.
    → She carefully read and examined it.**
    
    **The lawyer perused the contract before signing it.
    → The lawyer studied it carefully.**
    
- **nerve**
    
    *verb*
    
    to give courage or confidence
    
    🧠 nerve = nerve sounds like nervous but it’s opposite = confidence
    
    **She nerved herself to jump off the bungee.**
    
- **unnerve**
    
    *verb*
    
    to make nervous or diffident
    
    🧠 unnerve = un + nerve = nervous
    
- **retiring**
    
    *adjective*
    
    shy, quiet, and unwilling to attract attention; unsocial
    
    syn: **demure**; **shy; reserved; introverted**
    
    🧠 retiring = retired person are shy and reserved
    
- **demure**
    
    *adjective*
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH demur(*verb*)
    
    modest, shy, reserved, and behaving in a quiet, proper way.
    
    syn: **retiring; introverted**
    
    🧠 demure = actress demi moore = shy & modest
    
    **She gave a demure smile when praised.
    → A shy, modest smile.**
    
    **The actress appeared demure during the interview.
    → She behaved in a reserved, modest way.**
    
- **coy**
    
    *adjective*
    
    shy, modest, or pretending to be shy, often in a way that is meant to attract attention.
    
    syn: **retiring; timid**
    
    🧠 coy = sounds similar to shy
    
    🧠 **"Coy = Come on, You..."**
    
    Imagine someone asks:
    
    "Do you want the last piece of cake?"
    
    They secretly want it, but they say:
    
    "Oh no... I don't know... maybe..." 😏
    
    They are acting shy even though they want it.
    
    **Coy = pretending to be shy to be charming or playful.**
    
    **She gave a coy smile and avoided answering the question directly.**
    
    **The politician was coy about his future plans.**

- **telling**
    
    *adjective*
    
    revealing something important, significant, or providing evidence about a person or situation ⇒ one which makes it obvious/evident

    🧠 telling = i can tell = suggesting
    
    **Her telling reaction revealed that she already knew the answer.

    **The most telling sign of his success was the company's rapid growth.
