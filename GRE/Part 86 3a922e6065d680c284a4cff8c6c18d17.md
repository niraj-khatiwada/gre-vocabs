- **renege**
    
    *verb*
    
    to go back/withdraw on a promise, agreement, or commitment
    
    syn: **withdraw, retract, back out**
    
    🧠 renege = reject and go back
    
    🧠 renege = re + negate = to withdraw
    
    🤝 two people shaking hands on a deal
    
    then one person suddenly says:
    
    “Actually… never mind.”
    
    That person **reneged**.

- **moot**
    
    *noun*
    
    Meaning 1: something that is open to debate; not yet decided ⇒ debatable / arguable
    
    🧠 moot = meet and talk
    
    🧠 moot = we debate while mooting
    
    **Whether artificial intelligence will replace all jobs is still a moot question.
    → It is still open for debate.**
    
    **The effectiveness of the policy remains moot.
    → People still disagree about it.**
    
    Meaning 2: no longer important because it has no practical significance
    
    🧠 moot = imagine something is open to debate/not decided for several years ⇒ no longer significant
    
    **The lawsuit became moot after the law was repealed.**
    
    **→ There is no point deciding the case anymore.**
    
    ---
    
    *verb*
    
    moot 
    
    to raise or suggest an idea for discussion
    
    **She mooted a proposal to reduce taxes.**
    
    **→ She suggested it for discussion.**
    
- **accomplice**
    
    *noun*
    
    a person who helps another person commit a crime or wrongdoing.
    
    🧠 accomplice = a company that completes a wrongdoing
    
    🧠 accomplice = a partner in crime
    
    **The thief was arrested along with his accomplice.
    → The person who helped the thief.**
    
    **She was accused of being an accomplice to the fraud.
    → She helped someone carry out the fraud.**
