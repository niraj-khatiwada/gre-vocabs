# Part 64

- **abrogate**
    
    *verb*
    
    to officially cancel, abolish, or repeal a law, agreement, or obligation.
    
    syn: **axe; repudiate; repel; abolish**
    
    🧠 abrogate = abort + regulate = abolish + regulate
    
    **The government decided to abrogate the outdated law.**
    
- **aghast**
    
    *adjective*
    
    filled with shock, horror, or extreme surprise.
    
    syn: **dismayed; appalled**
    
    🧠 aghast = ah! ghost 😱
    
    **She was aghast when she heard the terrible news.**
    
- **apprise**
    
    *verb*
