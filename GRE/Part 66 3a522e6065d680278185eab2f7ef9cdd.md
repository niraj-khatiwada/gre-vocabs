- **abstruse**
    
    *adjective*

    syn: **recondite; esoteric**

- **dilettante**
    
    *noun*
    
    an amateur who dabbles without deep expertise.
    
    syn: **layman; amateur; dabbler; hobbyist**
    
    🧠 dilettante = de + literate = having little expertise or knowledge in a field
    
    🧠 dilettante = do little = an amateur
    
- **effrontery**
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH affront
    
    *noun*
    
    shameless boldness or extreme rudeness, especially when someone behaves in an offensively confident way.
    
    syn: **impudence; flippancy**
    
    🧠 effrontery  = confrontery = confronting someone with boldness and rudeness
    
    **He had the effrontery to demand a refund after damaging the product himself.**
    
    **The thief showed remarkable effrontery by returning to the same place.**

- **gregarious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    sociable, outgoing, and enjoying the company of other people.
    
    syn: **convivial; amiable**
    
    🧠 gregarious = great + gathering = sociable
    
    **Dolphins are known to be gregarious creatures.**
    
- **indignant**
    
    *adjective*
    
    feeling angry because you believe something is unfair or unjust
    
    syn: **resentful; vexed; aggrieved**
    
    🧠 indignant = in+dignity = someone treated unfair and hamper your dignity = angry due to such unjust behavior
    
    **She was indignant when she was accused of cheating.**
    
    **The citizens became indignant about the unfair treatment.**
    
- **aggrieved**
    
    *adjective*
    
    feeling sad, angry, and hurt because you believe someone has treated you unfairly or broken your rights
    
    syn: **indignant**
    
    🧠 aggrieved = verb form grieve
    
- **ineluctable**

    syn: i**nevitable; inexorable; inescapable; irrevocable; predestined**
    
    🧠 ineluctable = inevitable
    
- **inquisitive**
    
    *adjective*
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH acquisitive

    syn: **intrigued; nosy; prying; investigative**
    
    🧠 inquisitive = inquire+quisitive(question) = wants to know more
    
    🧠 inquisitive = inquire + active = wants to inquire more & more
