- **fungible**
    
    *adjective*
    
    assets that are interchangeable for another item
    
    syn: **exchangeable; equivalent; indistinguishable**
    
    🧠 fungible = remember NFT? Non Fungible Tokens = NFTs cannot be interchanged
    
    For eg: 1 dollar bill. We do not care which dollar bill we have. We just care about the value.
    
    But, for a NFT, the value is associated with that item itself. It cannot be exchanged with another one.
