- **bravado**
    
    *noun*
    
    a show of courage or confidence that is often exaggerated or fake, used to impress others or hide fear.
    
    syn: **swagger** (derived from swag); **machismo** (derived from macho)
    
    🧠 bravado = brave + show (pretentious) 
    
- **swagger**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: bravado
    
    🧠 swagger = swag
    
- **machismo**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **bravado; swagger**
    
    🧠 machismo = macho
    
- **callow**

    syn: **naive; guileless; artless; green**

- **dissonance**
    
    *noun*
    
    a lack of harmony, agreement, or compatibility.
    
    🧠 dissonance = discord+sonic(sound) = disagreement or lack of harmony in the discord voice chat
    
    syn: **discord**
    
    **There is a dissonance between his words and actions.
    → His words and actions do not match.**
    
    **The study revealed a dissonance between public opinion and government policy.
    → A disagreement/conflict.**
