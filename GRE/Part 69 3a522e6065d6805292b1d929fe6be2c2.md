- **culpability**
    
    *noun*
    
    a state of guilt, blameworthiness, or moral and legal responsibility for a wrongdoing
    
    syn: **blame, guilt, responsibility, fault, liability, accountability, wrongdoing**
    
    🧠 culpability = culprit + ability = guilt of wrongdoing
    
    **The investigation determined the driver's culpability in the accident.**
    
    **The report examined the company's culpability for the safety failures.**

- **glut**
    
    *noun/verb*
    
    noun: an excessive amount of something; oversupply
    
    verb: to supply or fill something excessively
    
    syn: **surplus; overflow; plethora; abundance**
    
    🧠 glut = great + a lot = excessive
    
    - **The market faced a glut of oil, causing prices to fall.
    → too much oil available**
    - **The store was glutted with unsold products.
    → filled with an excessive amount**

- **gall**
