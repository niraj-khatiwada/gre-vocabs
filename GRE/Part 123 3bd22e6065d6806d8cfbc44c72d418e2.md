# Part 123

- **apoplectic**
    
    *adjective*
    
    extremely angry; furious; overcome with rage
    
    Core Idea: rage so intense that your face turns red and you look ready to explode.
    
    **If M**i put price controls on product like pharmaceuticals, if he bailed out farms, if he took stakes in private companies, if he said the energy companies are making too much money, if he proposed credit card caps-you would all go *apoplectic*, as would your viewers.**

    *noun*
    
    apoplexy
    
- **indispensable**
    
    *adjective*
    
    absolutely necessary
    
    antonym: **dispensable → superfluous; unnecessary**
    
    🧠
    
    - **in-** = not
    - **dispensable** = able to be removed / done without
    
    👉 **indispensable = cannot be done without**
    
    **“Technology has become indispensable part of our lives.”**
    
    **“an indispensable employee”**
    
- **toil**
    
    *verb*
    
    exhausting, physical or mental labor; work hard
    
    **Nobody is going to save you. You need to toil all day long to achieve success.**
    
    🧠 toil = something that takes a toll = exhausting
    
- **precipice**
    
    *noun*
    
    very steep cliff or the edge of a dangerous drop
    
    Core Idea: in the brink of disaster, danger, or a major change.
    
    🧠 precipice = pre+price to pay = danger before paying the price
