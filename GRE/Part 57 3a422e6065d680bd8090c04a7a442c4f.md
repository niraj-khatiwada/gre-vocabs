# Part 57

- **miscreant**
    
    *noun*
    
    a person who behaves badly or breaks the law; a wrongdoer or villain.
    
    syn: **malefactor; culprit; delinquent**
    
    🧠 miscreant = mis-creates problem = breaks the law
    
    ![image.png](Part%2057/image.png)
    
- **delinquent**
    
    *noun*
    
    **meaning 1: negligent / overdue**
    
    **A delinquent payment → a payment that is late or unpaid**
    
    - **young offender**
        
        **A juvenile delinquent → a young person who breaks the law**
        
        syn: **miscreant**
        
    
    🧠 delinquent = delay + neglect
    
- **peccadillo**
    
    *noun*
    
    a small fault, minor mistake, or insignificant offense.
    
    syn: **misdemeanor; misdeed; slip**
    
    🧠 peccadillo = peculiar + dillo (mistake)
    
- **philistine**
    
    *adjective*
    
    a person who is uncultured, lacks appreciation for art, literature, or intellectual ideas, and cares mainly about practical/material things.
    
    syn: **materialistic**
    
    🧠 philistine = anti + philosophy = materialistic
