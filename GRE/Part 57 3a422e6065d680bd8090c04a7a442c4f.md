# Part 57

- **miscreant**
    
    *noun*
    
    a person who behaves badly or breaks the law; a wrongdoer or villain.
    
    syn: **malefactor; culprit; delinquent**
    
    🧠 miscreant = mis-creates problem = breaks the law
    
    ![image.png](Part%2057/image.png)
    
- **malefactor**
    
    *noun*
    
    a person who breaks the law
    
    🧠 malefactor = mal+factor = doesn’t factor(solve) anything and breaks the law
    
- **delinquent**
    
    *noun*
    
    Meaning 1: negligent / overdue
    
    **A delinquent payment → a payment that is late or unpaid**
    
    Meaning 2: young offender
    
    **A juvenile delinquent → a young person who breaks the law**
    
    syn: **miscreant**
    
    🧠 delinquent = delay + neglect
    
- **peccadillo**
    
    *noun*
    
    a small fault, minor mistake, or insignificant offense.
    
    syn: **misdemeanor; misdeed; slip**
    
    🧠 peccadillo = peculiar + dillo (mistake)
    
- **philistine**
    
    *adjective*
    
    a person who is uncultured, lacks appreciation for art, literature, or intellectual ideas, and cares mainly about practical/material things.
    
    syn: **materialistic**
    
    🧠 philistine = anti + philosophy = materialistic
    
- **delegate**
    
    *verb*
    
    give an assignment to (a person)
    
- **relegate**
    
    *verb*
    
    to assign to lower position
    
- **repugnant**
    
    *adjective*
    
    loathsome; extreme dislike; repulsive
    
    syn: **odious**; **abhorrent; loathsome; repellent**
    
    ![image.png](Part%2057/image%201.png)
    
- **odious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    extremely unpleasant, offensive, or deserving strong dislike.
    
    syn: **repugnant; detestable; abhorrent; loathsome**
    
    🧠 odious = imagine very disgusting odor = repugnant
    
- **swindle**
    
    *verb*
    
    deceive someone
    
    syn: **dupe; deceive; hoax**
    
- **tangible**
    
    *adjective*
    
    something that can be touched
    
    syn: **palpable; tactile; perceptible**
    
- **turpitude**
    
    *noun*
    
    moral corruption, wickedness, or extremely shameful behavior; a depraved attitude
    
    syn: **immorality; wickedness; villainy**
    
    🧠 turpitude = terrible attitude
    
    ![image.png](Part%2057/image%202.png)
    
- **unalloyed**
    
    pure; without any adulterations
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **genuine; authentic; pristine**
    
    🧠 unalloyed = alloy is a mix of chemicals and a metal ⇒ unalloyed means no mix = 100% pure
    
- **undercut**
    
    *verb*
    
    Meaning 1: undermine / weaken
    
    **His actions undercut/undermine his credibility.**
    
    ⇒ His actions made people trust him less.
    
    Meaning 2: sell cheaper than others
    
    **The company undercut its competitors by lowering prices.**
    
    ⇒ subvert
    
    syn: **subvert**
