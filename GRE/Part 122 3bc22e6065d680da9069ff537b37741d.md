- **quagmire**

    Figuratively**:** a complicated, difficult situation that is hard to get out of.
    
    🧠 quagmire = quick mud = mud that sinks you quickly = difficult to get out of

- **quell**

- **unctuous**
    
    *adjective*
    
    excessively flattering or overly smooth (usually to get something)
    
    syn: **ingratiating; obsequious; fawning**
    
    🧠 unctuous = uncle+obsequious = uncle wants something via flattery
    
    Imagine an **uncle** who is ridiculously flattering
    
    > “Ohhh, you're absolutely brilliant! What an amazing idea! You're the greatest!”
    > 
    
    But you can tell he wants a favor.
    
    **The politician's unctuous compliments seemed designed to win the wealthy donor's support.**
    
    **The salesperson adopted an unctuous tone that made his enthusiasm seem completely insincere.**
    
    **His unctuous manner irritated his colleagues, who suspected that his excessive politeness concealed ulterior motives.**
    
- **unsurp**
    
    *verb*
    
    to seize a position, power, or authority that rightfully belongs to someone else
    
    Core Idea: to take power that isn’t yours
    
    🧠 unsurp = u sir, up? this is my sit = forcefully take the position
    
    **The military leader attempted to usurp the president's authority and take control of the government.**

    **In his valedictory address, the president thanked the citizens who had supported him.**
    
    **The CEO's valedictory remarks emphasized the company's achievements and future challenges**
