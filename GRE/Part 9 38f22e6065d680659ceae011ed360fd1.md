- **cosmopolitan**
    
    *adjective*
    
    highly diverse; worldly; international; traveled around the world
    
    🧠 cosmopolitan magazine
    
- **deference**
    
    *noun*
    
    high respect or esteem towards someone
    
    Showing respect by yielding to someone, often because of their age, status, expertise, or position.
    
    syn: **reverence, obeisance**
    
    🧠 defer → deference = defer means to yield, so we yield towards someone out of respect
    
    ---
    
    defer = verb
    
    Meaning 1: to delay or postpone something to later time
    
    Meaning 2: to yield to another person out of respect
