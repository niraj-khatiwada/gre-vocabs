- **slake**
    
    *verb*
    
    to satisfy a thirst, desire, or need
    
    syn: **quench**
    
    🧠 slake = satisfy+lake 💦 = you satisfy your thirst on the lake
    
    **The traveler drank water to slake his thirst.
    → To satisfy his thirst.**
    
    **She read books to slake her curiosity.
    → To satisfy her curiosity.**
    
    **The company expanded to slake consumer demand.
    → To satisfy demand.**
    
- **quench**
    
    *verb*
    
    to satisfy a thirst, desire, or need 
    
    syn: **slake**
    
    🧠 quench = sounds like quoosh = suppress = satisfy = when you’re satisfying your thirst, it is suppressing your thirst
    
    ***“quench your thirst”***
    
    **She drank water to quench her thirst.
    → Satisfy thirst.**
    
    **Firefighters quenched the flames.
    → Extinguished the fire.**
    
    **He tried to quench his curiosity.
    → Suppress/satisfy curiosity.**
