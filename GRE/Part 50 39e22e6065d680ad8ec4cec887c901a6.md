- **nonplussed**
    
    *adjective*
    
    surprised, confused, or unsure how to react.
    
    syn: **flummoxed**; **bewildered; baffled; perplexed; bemused**
    
    🧠 nonplussed
    
    **"Nonplussed = No + Plus"**
    
    Imagine:
    
    You are solving a math problem and suddenly there is **no plus (+) sign anywhere**.
    
    Your brain freezes:
    
    "What do I do now?"
    
    You are confused and unable to react. ****⇒ You are **nonplussed.**
