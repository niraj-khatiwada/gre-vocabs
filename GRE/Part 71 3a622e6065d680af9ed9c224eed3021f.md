- **languid**
    
    *adjective*

- **disaffected**
    
    *adjective*
    
    dissatisfied with or no longer loyal to a person, organization, or government.
    
    Core idea: you've lost your support or loyalty because you're unhappy.
    
    syn: **alienated; estranged; disenchanted**
    
    **Many disaffected voters refused to support either candidate.**
    
    **The government tried to win back its disaffected citizens.**
    
    🧠 disaffected = not affected to you anymore = lost loyalty = dissatisfied
    
    Think of an employee.
    
    At first:
    
    😊 "I love this company!"
    
    After years of bad treatment:
    
    😐 "I don't care anymore. I'm done with this company."
    
    The company no longer **affects** their loyalty.
    
    They become **disaffected**.
    
- **discontented**
    
    *adjective*
    
    not satisfied; unhappy with one's situation, conditions, or circumstances.
    
    syn: **disaffected; displeased; resentful**
    
    🧠 discontented = dis + content (content means happy) = not happy
    
- **quotidian**
    
    *adjective*
    
    means ordinary, everyday, or occurring regularly.
    
    Core idea: something that's part of your daily routine or completely ordinary.
    
    syn: **ordinary; routine; commonplace; habitual; mundane**
    
    🧠 quotidian 
    
    "Quote of the Day"
    
    Imagine you receive a "Quote of the Day" every morning.
    
    It happens every single day.
    
    So think:
    
    Quote every day → Quotidian = daily/everyday.
    
    **The novel finds beauty in quotidian life.**
    
    **She was tired of the quotidian routine of commuting and office work.**

- **refractory**
    
    *adjective*
    
    stubbornly resistant to control, treatment, or change.
    
    - stubborn / difficult to manage
        
        **A refractory child refused to follow instructions. ⇒ *jiddi***
        
    - resistant to treatment (especially medical or punishment)
        
        **The disease was refractory to available drugs.**
        
        syn: **incorrigible**; **adamant; obstinate; obdurate; dogmatic; tenacious; unyielding**
        
        🧠 refractory = refraction = is very stubborn and refracts away = bent away 
        
- **incorrigible**
    
    *adjective*
    
    impossible to correct, reform, or improve, especially because of repeated bad behavior; not able to correct someone even after repeated punishment
    
    syn: **refractory**;  **irreformable; unmanageable; hopeless**
    
    ant: **corrigible**
    
    🧠 incorrigible = in-correctable
    
    **The teacher called him an incorrigible troublemaker.**
    
    **Despite repeated warnings, the incorrigible liar continued to deceive everyone.**
    
- **corrigible**
