- **ostracize**
    
    *verb*
    
    to exclude, reject, or deliberately keep someone away from a group or society.
    
    syn; **shun; secluded; banish; alienate; insular**
    
    🧠 ostracize = imagine ostrich are placed separately in a zoo because they attack people = isolated
    
- **insularize**
    
    *verb*
    
    to isolate from a group
    
    syn: **ostracize**
    
- **intrepid**
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH insipid
    
    *adjective*
    
    fearless; brave; courageous; bold
    
    🧠 trepid = derived from word **trepidation** which literally means a feeling of fear, anxiety or worry
    
    🧠 intrepid = opposite of trepid(trepid means timid, fearful, apprehensive) = fearless
    
- **plucky**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **intrepid; brave**
    
    🧠 plucky = pluck the bravery
    
- **foment**
    
    *verb*
    
    To stir up, encourage, or provoke something—especially something negative like trouble, conflict, rebellion, or unrest.
    
    syn: **incite; agitate; inflame; provoke**
    
    🧠 foment = fume+moment = inflame = incite
    
- **content**
    
    *adjective*
    
    feeling satisfied and happy
    
    🧠 content = complete
    
- **hegemony**
    
    *noun*
    
    dominance or control over certain area or something
    
    syn: **dominance; authority; supremacy; control**
    
    🧠 hegemony = derived from **hegemon** which means a dominant ruler
    
    **The empire maintained hegemony over the region for centuries.**
    
    **The country gained cultural hegemony through its movies, language, and media.**

    **“Tigers are prolific breeders.”**
