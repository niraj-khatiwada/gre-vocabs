# Part 78

- **tacit**
    
    *adjective*
    
    understood or implied without being directly stated or spoken.
    
    syn: **implicit; indirect; implied**
    
    🧠 tacit = we know that taciturn means reserved(not speaking) = so tacit means understood without speaking or understood by implying instead of stating
    
    **There was a tacit agreement between the two companies.
    → They agreed without formally saying it.**
    
    **She gave her tacit approval by remaining silent.
    → She did not say yes, but her actions suggested approval.**
    
- **divest**
    
    *verb*
    
    to pull out or sell your assets
    
    🧠 divest = opposite of invest
    
- **propriety**
    
    *noun*
    
    DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH proprietary
    
    conforming to a standard; appropriateness; behavior that follows accepted morals
    
    syn: **decorum; etiquette; morality; appropriateness**
    
    🧠 propriety = proper + iety = conforming to standard
    
- **opprobrium**
    
    severe criticism; public shame
    
    syn: **castigation; vituperation; vilification**
    
    🧠 opprobrium = oh problem = criticism
    
    🧠 opprobrium = oppose = criticize
    
- **eulogy**
    
    *noun*
    
    a speech or written tribute praising someone, especially someone who has died.
    
    syn: **tribute; praise; homage; commendation; accolade**
    
    **The son delivered a moving eulogy at his father's funeral.**
    
    **The president gave a eulogy honoring the famous scientist.**
    
- **explicit**
    
    *adjective*
    
    clearly and directly stated, leaving no room for confusion or doubt.
    
    **The teacher gave explicit instructions for the assignment.
    → The instructions were very clear.**
    
    **The contract contains explicit rules about payment.
    → The rules are directly written.**
    
- **fervor**
    
    *noun*
    
    an intense passion or enthusiasm towards something
    
    syn: zeal; ardor; passion
    
    🧠 fervor = forever passion

    ardor → ardent
    
    zeal → zealous
