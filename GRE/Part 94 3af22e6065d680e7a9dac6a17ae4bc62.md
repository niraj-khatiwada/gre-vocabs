- **surmise**
    
    *verb*
    
    form an opinion without absolute proof; to guess based on limited evidence
    
    syn: **presume; assume**
    
    🧠 surmise = suppose+guess
    
    🧠 **surm**ise = **sur**e+**m**aybe = guess without evidence
    
- **conjecture**
    
    *noun/verb*

    syn: **speculate**

- **watershed**
    
    *noun*
    
    a turning point or a major event that marks a significant change ⇒ a dividing point where things change direction.
    
    🧠 watershed = water divides higher and lower point
    
    **The invention of the internet was a watershed moment in human history.
    → It was a major turning point.**
    
    **The election marked a watershed in the country's political development.
    → It created a significant change.**
    
    **The discovery was a watershed achievement for medical science.
    → It changed the field dramatically.**

- **rarefy**
    
    *verb*
    
    to make something thin, less dense ⇒ to make something rare
    
    **To rarefy the air in the altitude chamber, the scientists used a powerful pump.**
    
- **permeate**
    
    *verb*
    
    to spread throughout
    
    🧠 permeate = remember impermeable? impermeable means to not be able to penetrate or go through something; so permeate means able to penetrate or go through the small pores or opening and spread out
    
    **The scent of freshly baked bread began to permeate the entire house.**
    
- **euphemism**
    
    *noun*
    
    a polite term used to substitute a harsh one
    
    | Euphemism | Actual Meaning |
    | --- | --- |
    | passed away | died |
    | let go | fired |
    | senior citizen | old person |
    | correctional facility | prison |
    | economically disadvantaged | poor |
    
    syn: **circumlocution; softening; underplaying**
    
- **circumlocution**
    
    *noun*
    
    use of lengthy (indirect) words to describe an idea
    
    syn: **euphemism; equivocation**
    
    🧠 circumlocution = circumference + location = using lengthy words to describe a location
    
    ***“the small, round object used for bouncing in games" instead of just saying "ball”***
