# Part 54

- **ardent**
    
    *adjective*
    
    having a lot of passion and enthusiasm
    
    syn: **zealous; fervid; fervent;**
    
- **zealous**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **ardent;** 
    
- **fervid**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **ardent; zealous**
    
- **fervent**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **ardent; zealous; fervid**
    
- **cessation**
    
    *noun*
    
    stop something from happening
    
    🧠 cessation = derived from word cease
    
    🧠 incessant = in + cessation = not ceasing = constant, not stopping; but cessation means to stop
    
- **corporeal**
    
    *adjective*
    
    associated with physical body; physical material rather than spiritual
    
    syn: **bodily; fleshly**
    
    🧠 corporeal = corpse + relation = relating to physical body
    
    **The soul is different from our corporeal existence.**
    
- **doctrinaire**
    
    *adjective*
    
    completely devoted to the doctrine belief or theory
    
    syn: **dogmatic; opinionated; fanatical**
    
    🧠 doctrinaire = person believing firmly on the their doctrine
    
- **equanimity**
    
    *noun*
    
    mental calmness, composure, and emotional stability
    
    syn: **poise; serenity; tranquility**
    
    🧠 equanimity = equal mind = balanced mind

- **fickle**
    
    *adjective*
    
    changing opinions or feelings frequently and unpredictably
    
    syn: **mercurial; capricious; indecisiveness; volatile; erratic**
    
    🧠 fickle = flip opinions
    
- **capricious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **fickle; mercurial**
    
- **mercurial**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **fickle; capricious**
    
- **flustered**
    
    *adjective*
    
    confused
    
    syn: **baffled; bewildered; perplexed**
