# Part 60

- **abscond**
    
    *verb*
    
    to depart suddenly and secretly, especially to avoid arrest, prosecution, or legal obligations.
    
    syn: **flee; bolt**
    
    **The thief absconded the crime scene.**
    
- **apogee**
    
    *noun*
    
    the highest point, peak, or greatest level of achievement or success.
    
    syn: **zenith; apex; acme; culminating**
    
    ant: **nadir**
    
    🧠 apogee = a peak = highest point
    
- **nadir**
    
    *noun*
    
    the lowest point or level of achievement
    
    syn: **rock bottom; abyss**
    
    ant: **zenith; apogee**
    
- **aspersion**
    
    *noun*
    
    a damaging remark, accusation, or criticism that harms someone's reputation.
    
    syn: **vilification; smear; defamation; denigration; slander**
    
    🧠 aspersion = a spear thrown at someone's reputation = defamation
    
- **bawdy**
    
    *adjective*
    
    humor or language that is openly sexual, vulgar, or related to bodily matters, often in a joking way.
    
    syn: **lewd; titillating**
    
    🧠 bawdy = body humor
    
    **The writer was known for his bawdy comedies, which mixed clever satire with crude humor.**
    
- **chagrin**
    
    *noun/verb*
    
    a feeling of embarrassment, disappointment, or distress caused by failure or a mistake ⇒  embarrassing disappointment
    
    syn: **resentment; vexation; humiliation**
    
    🧠 chagrin = shame + grin

    *noun*

    syn: **inferno; blaze; firestorm**

- **contretemps**
    
    *noun*
    
    a minor, unexpected, or embarrassing mishap, disagreement, or awkward situation (It often happens publicly and causes temporary social awkwardness)
    
    syn: **quarrel; squabble**
    
    🧠 contretemps = contre + temper = counter temper = disagreement
    
- **conviction**
    
    *noun*
    
    1. A strong belief or firmly held opinion
    
    A belief that you are very certain about.
    
    Example: *She spoke with great conviction about the importance of education.*
    
    2. A legal judgment that someone is guilty of a crime
    
    The result of being officially found guilty.
    
    syn: **stance; principle**
    
- **croon**
    
    *verb*
    
    to sing or speak in a soft, gentle, low voice
    
    syn: **serenade; hum**
    
    🧠 croon
    
    **"Croon sounds like 'coo'"**
    
    A dove says:
    
    "Coo... coo..."
    
    A soft, gentle sound.
    
- **depose**
    
    *verb*
    
    forcibly remove someone from power
    
    syn: **axe; expel; oust**
    
    🧠 depose = de + position = expel
    
- **detente**
    
    *noun*
    
    a relaxation of tension or hostility between countries, groups, or individuals.
    
    syn: **pacification; conciliation**
    
    🧠 detente = de-tension
    
- **dowdy**
    
    *adjective*
    
    lacking style or elegance
    
    syn: **shabby; frumpy; frumpish**
    
    🧠 dowdy = sounds like gaudy = gaudy means overly decorated like over-makeup = lacking style
