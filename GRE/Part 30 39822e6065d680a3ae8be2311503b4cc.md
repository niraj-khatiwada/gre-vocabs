# Part 30

- **futile**
    
    *adjective*
    
    no possibility of success; won’t happen; unproductive
    
    syn: **fruitless; ineffectual; vain**
    
    🧠 futile = futxa = no success
    
    **Your quest to success in life is futile.**
    
    ---
    
    *noun*
    
    futility
    
- **illusory**
    
    *adjective*
    
    based on illusion; unreal
    
    syn: **delusional; bogus; sham**
    
- **invidious**
    
    *adjective*
    
    likely to cause resentment, envy, or offense because it is unfair, discriminatory
    
    Core idea: an unfair comparison that makes people feel bad
    
    syn: **offensive; prejudicial; unfair; divisive; biased; unpleasant; resentful; undesirable** 
    
    🧠 invidious = envy + hideous(hidden) = hidden envy due to unfairness
    
- **numinous**
    
    *adjective*
    
    having spiritual or divine quality
    
    syn: **sacred; otherwordly; transcendental ; mystical; spiritual; supernatural; divine**
    
    🧠 numinous = nama bhagwan
    
- **transcendental**
    
    *adjective*
    
    anything beyond ordinary human experience; out of the world; beyond reality
    
    syn: **numinous**; **otherwordly; mystical**
    
    🧠 transcendental = derived from word transcend = go beyond ordinary limit
    
    🧠 transcendental = transcend out of reality 
    
- **pellucid**
    
    *adjective*
    
    transparent; clear
    
    syn: **limpid; unambiguous**
    
    🧠 pel**lucid** = lucid
    
- **superficial**
    
    *adjective*
    
    not deep; touched surfacely; not thorough; not enough care
    
    syn: **cursory; desultory; perfunctory**
    
- **eclectic**
    
    *adjective*
    
    collecting ideas from multiple sources
    
    Core idea: Mix of different things
    
    🧠 eclectic = elective = you have multiple options/sources
    
- **morph**
    
    *verb*
    
    to undergo drastic change
