- **nonplussed**
    
    *adjective*
    
    surprised, confused, or unsure how to react.
    
    syn: **flummoxed**; **bewildered; baffled; perplexed; bemused**
    
    🧠 nonplussed
    
    **"Nonplussed = No + Plus"**
    
    Imagine:
    
    You are solving a math problem and suddenly there is **no plus (+) sign anywhere**.
    
    Your brain freezes:
    
    "What do I do now?"
    
    You are confused and unable to react. ****⇒ You are **nonplussed.**

- **postulate**
    
    *verb*
    
    assume something as true even without full proof; use the available evidence as a starting point to prove in the future
    Core idea: **assume first → prove later;** done by scientist and scholars
    
    syn: **posit;** **hypothesize; assume; theorize**
    
    🧠 postulate = post it note 📌 
    
    You write an idea on a **Post-it note** and stick it on the wall.
    
    You haven't proved it yet.
    
    You're saying:
    
    > "Let's assume this is true and build from here."
    > 
    
    👉 That's a **postulate**.
