# Part 58

- **abeyance**
    
    *noun*
    
    a temporary state of suspension, inactivity, or postponement.
    
    syn: **moratorium; dormancy; hiatus; remission (re+mission = we’ll start mission soon, right now it’s paused)**
    
    🧠 abeyance = a+bay+yacht = waiting for yacht = inactive state
    
    **"A BAY = Waiting at the Bay"**
    
    Break it into:
    
    **A + BAY + ance**
    
    Imagine a ship 🚢 arriving at a **bay**, but the harbor master says:
    
    **"Wait here. Don't dock yet."**
    
    The ship isn't canceled—it is just **waiting**.
    
    **Waiting in the bay = Abeyance = temporarily on hold.**

    "Do you want the last piece of cake?"

    "Oh no... I don't know... maybe..." 😏

    **Coy = pretending to be shy to be charming or playful.**
    
    **She gave a coy smile and avoided answering the question directly.**
