# Part 7

- **abate**
    
    *verb*
    
    to become less strong or to reduce in intensity, amount, or value
    
    syn: diminish; **wane;** lessen
    
    🧠 abate = a bit less
    
- **subside**
    
    *verb*
    
    syn: **abate**
    
    🧠 **sub = decrease**
    
- **abjure**
    
    *verb*
    
    to reject or renounce (an idea)
    
    syn: **renounce**; **disavow; forsake**
    
    🧠 ab + jury ⇒ “I object, Your Honor!”
    
- **objurgate**
    
    *verb*
    
    express strong disapproval of
    
    syn: **adjure; disavow**
    
    🧠 objurgate = abjure
    
    **The manager spent an hour objurgating the employee in the hopes that he would not make these mistakes again.**
    
- **disavow**
    
    *verb*
    
    syn: abjure
    
    🧠 vow = support; disavow = reject
    
- **forsake**
    
    *verb*
    
    to abandon, completely give up
    
    syn: **abjure; disavow**
    
- **anomalous**
    
    *adjective*
    
    differing from normal or standard
    
    syn: **atypical; abnormal**
    
    🧠 anomaly = differences

- **antipathy**
    
    **DO NOT GET CONFUSED WITH “apathy”**
    
    *noun*
    
    A strong feeling of dislike, hatred, hostility, or deep aversion 
    
    syn: **aversion; repugnance**
    
    🧠 
    
    - **Anti** = against
    - **pathy** = feeling (as in sympathy, empathy)
- **animosity**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **antipathy**
    
- **repugnance**
    
    *adjective*
    
    syn: **antipathy; animosity**
    
- **apathy**
    
    *noun*
    
    lack of emotion, feeling, interest, enthusiasm
    
    syn: **indifference**
    
    🧠 apathy = a + pathy = no emotion
    
    ---
    
    🧠 antipathy = anti + emotion = against emotion = hatred
