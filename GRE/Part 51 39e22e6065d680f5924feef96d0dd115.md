- **condone**
    
    *verb*
    
    accept, approve or support something that is considered wrong or illegal
    
    syn: **disregard; pardon; let-slide**
    
    condemn vs condone:
    
    condemn means to strongly disapprove of or criticize something as wrong
    
    condone means to overlook, forgive, or allow behavior that is generally considered unacceptable.
    
    ***That is completely unacceptable," while to condone is to say, "It's okay, I'll let it slide.***

- **quiescent**
    
    NOT RELATED TO acquiescent
    
    *adjective*
    
    quiet; inactive; at rest
    
    syn: **dormant; latent**
    
    🧠 quiescent = quiet+ascent = no ascent for now = inactive right now
    
    **The volcano has been quiescent for centuries.
    → It is inactive but could erupt again.**
    
    **The market remained quiescent during the holiday.
    → It was quiet and inactive.**
    
    **The disease can remain quiescent for years.
    → It stays dormant/inactive.**
